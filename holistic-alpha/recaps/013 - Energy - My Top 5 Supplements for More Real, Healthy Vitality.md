> **TL;DR** — Supplements don't make up for the fundamentals, which get you 70 to 80% of the way to the energy you want; the top five for healthy energy are coffee, acetyl-L-carnitine, alpha GPC, cordyceps and shilajit, bought with an eye on quality, especially shilajit.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 13, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Ground rule: the **fundamentals** first, since supplements won't make up for not doing them. For energy, and really wellness: enough high quality sleep, a decent diet that fuels your body effectively, regular exercise, not wasting your sex energy, and meditating to keep stress in check. With the basics in place you're 70 to 80% of the way to the energy you desire; supplements can take us the rest of the way
- **Story:** the last decade went into taking dozens and dozens of supplements and researching, a lot of it around energy, since a lot of us are looking for more energy in our lives. Back in the day: energy drinks, and feeling absolutely horrible from them; years of mistakes and dialing things in followed
- The personal top five for real, healthy energy, in no particular order, or maybe in a particular order in this case:
  - **Coffee**, amazing for energy, in a healthy way, and for hell's sake not soda: a morning can of soda is a completely different thing, and so is tea
    - For years a lot of people looked at coffee as maybe a vice, but more and more science has come to show it's very, very good for us, with a number of really well-proven benefits beyond energy
    - More mental and physical energy and a faster brain, at a moderate amount: a couple cups, not eight shots of espresso. A study shows a couple cups can cut post-workout muscle pain by up to 48%
    - Some of the other scientifically proven benefits: associated with a lower risk of diabetes, Alzheimer's, MS and colorectal cancer; a really large study shows it reduces suicide risk and depression; studies show it protects against Parkinson's; coffee drinkers have shown in studies a lower risk of heart disease, and DNA of stronger integrity
    - It can make you feel great and get more done, and seems to have a place in every adult's life, and probably a lot of teenagers could benefit from a moderate amount. In your life it most definitely has a place: a huge source of energy beyond the basics
  - **Acetyl-L-carnitine**, maybe not heard of, like the rest of the list: an amino acid; carnitine is produced naturally in our body
    - Its big role is with our mitochondria, absolutely crucial to so much of our health, wellness and energy, with a lot of science around them now. On a cellular level they have a huge role in helping our body run effectively, so acetyl-L-carnitine, by essentially boosting them, basically boosts one of the most important aspects of virtually every cell. Almost every cell has them, and one of their major roles is energy production, so boosting them boosts energy on a cellular level
    - Of the forms of carnitine, it crosses the blood-brain barrier into the brain's mitochondria, a dense source of them and a really important area to fuel: it impacts both mental and physical energy directly
    - It ramps up energy production, increases endurance and boosts the mitochondria's ability to burn fat, both for losing fat and for using fat as fuel, "which is the most effective fuel for our body"
    - Absolutely one of the personal favorites, relatively inexpensive: personally, one to really try not to be without, other than just occasionally cycling off. Used by our body at such a fundamental level, it seems to have a long-term place in our lives
  - **Alpha GPC**, a source of choline, in simple terms essentially fuel in our brain, and critical: a really important nutrient we need. It comes from diet, like eggs, but a lot of us are deficient, and supplementing has some amazing benefits
    - Brain and body are so connected, and there's a lot of science around things like increased power output. Personally, a tremendous physical impact in addition to fueling the brain: a tremendous overall energy boost, in a healthy way
    - Of the forms, such as CDP choline, alpha GPC has personally been the best
    - Timing: it tends to take about two hours for maximum benefits, so take it a couple hours before your workout. The other four take effect within about 15 to 30 minutes, once digested, a great window pre-workout. **Story:** all or most of these, depending on what is being short-term cycled off, make the pre-workout mix on an almost daily basis
  - **Cordyceps**, a mushroom. Of the ton of beneficial mushrooms, like reishi and lion's mane, it's really the most well known for physical sort of power and energy
    - Personally, nothing gets taken or recommended now without science behind it. Cordyceps is a really, truly ancient medicine, used by humans for a really long time, with a lot of modern science too
    - Unlike alpha GPC, a very specific nutrient, it has a little more wide-ranging impacts, as adaptogens and mushrooms benefit the body in multiple ways. A main one is energy on a fundamental, cellular kind of level: it has been shown very effective at boosting ATP, a molecule really essential for delivering energy to our body. Lots of studies show VO2 max, aerobic ability, improving with it, and there are studies on anti-aging and anti-cancer properties
    - Personally, truly increased physical energy and endurance: doing more and feeling better, day after day, on it than off it
    - Another favorite: it really increases lung function noticeably, with some science, and most definitely noticed personally: breathing is naturally deeper and stronger, sometimes striking when restarting after cycling off. For any breathing, lung or congestion issue, cordyceps is amazing
  - **Shilajit**, very likely not heard of: an almost tar-like, sticky substance that squeezes out of rocks in the mountains, primarily the Himalayas, basically really, really old plant material condensed and treated by the earth over time
    - Highly nutrient dense, with 80-plus minerals, so you only need a really, really small amount, and fulvic acid, of which it is pretty much the best source: an antioxidant that helps the body in a number of ways, including getting rid of toxins, fighting off reactions that cause allergy symptoms, reducing inflammation and preventing the growth of cancer, and it stimulates the immune system: it does lots of things in our body
    - **Quality**: shilajit has a number of good brands, but a suggestion: be a little bit careful, as shilajit is one of those to be careful with, and cordyceps is kind of also this way. Definitely look for quality cordyceps: there are a number of good quality companies, so pay attention that you actually buy one of those. For alpha GPC, acetyl-L-carnitine and coffee, pretty much most companies are relatively good, though look for organic coffee when you can. With shilajit, spend some extra time: there's a lot of lower quality out there, especially blends that supposedly include it, which often have very poor quality or not much of it
    - Probably different from anything you've ever taken, and maybe the most powerful supplement personally taken in terms of truly feeling it: within a day, or a moderate amount the first time, it's really powerful for overall energy
    - A lot of science on it: it benefits and reduces Alzheimer's disease, improves low testosterone and chronic fatigue, is used against high altitude sickness, helps fight iron deficiencies, improves fertility and heart health. Personally, powerful energy benefits: highly suggested
- The top five found in all the research and experimentation, with an invitation to try any or all: "They're all safe and awesome and I think would benefit you"
