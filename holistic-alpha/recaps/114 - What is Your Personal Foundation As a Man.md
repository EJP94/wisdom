> **TL;DR** — Your personal foundation is the simple daily habits across the five pillars of power, such as water, daily grinding with push-ups and squats, meditation, Kegels, semen retention and cold showers: done every day they carry you forward, and without them it all crumbles.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 114, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- What are the foundational habits that carry you forward? Can you instantly point to the things you do as part of every day? If you can't, that's a problem, because it adds up over time: without those fundamentals in place every day, we go nowhere, or certainly not where we want to go
- Your **personal foundation** is the habits you implement every day in the five pillars of power: fitness, nutrition, hormone optimization, sex energy optimization and mind. They are pillars because without any one of them the foundation will crumble over time: do everything else right but neglect your mind, for example, and it all comes down. We need all five
- Some examples, not a prescription of this and this every day, but foundational things that really support you:
  - **Water** is so huge. **Story:** for the guys going through Bootcamp right now, water was one of the very first topics, on day one. Dehydrated, your energy, decision making and workouts suffer: a trickle-down effect
  - **Physical grinding every day.** **Story:** the Bootcamp guys do push-ups and squats every single day. We need strong bodies and minds, and to remind ourselves daily that we can do hard things. A suggestion: no matter what else you do, do push-ups and squats. It sounds so simple, but make it part of your foundation and things change
    - Rest days occasionally, yes, but grind every day. Not a little bit sore from day to day means not pushing hard enough; super sore maybe means you pushed a little too hard or didn't recover enough
    - Without the physical work, that part of the foundation is weak, and if it's not causing problems yet, it will: we cannot build the life we want without a complete foundation
  - **Meditating**: if we don't take care of our mind, whatever else we do, it all crumbles. Meditation is one of the best ways to take care of, understand and connect with our mind, connect with others, de-stress, on and on. If it's not part of your foundation, make it part: it will truly benefit your life
  - **Kegels**, every day: we need to exercise our sexual core just like the rest of our body. Without that, the effects kind of add up and the foundation of your sex life kind of falls apart. It's not just dependent on that, but we need that physical strength
  - **Building your sexual energy**, which you can build day by day: the habits around it are hugely impactful. Without semen retention in your foundation, you're leaving a lot on the table. "I truly believe" you will not be able to experience, achieve or be what you want while giving up your sexual energy: "it doesn't work". Conserve and build it instead of wasting it
  - Maybe one of the last, "I think": **do hard things**. Achieving what you haven't yet, new things, growing: life is hard, not about easy. We need to remind ourselves every day that we can do hard things and come back to that deeper, stronger place. Physical grinding is a great way, but one habit is so beneficial for it, one the Bootcamp guys do too: cold showers
    - A cold shower is choosing the uncomfortable thing in the moment because you know it leads where you want to go. On the physical and mental benefits, "the science is there", and anybody who's tried it will tell you they're so worth it
    - But the brief discomfort is the best part, and seemingly why cold showers should be part of your foundation: every day they remind you that you can do hard things, to look discomfort in the face and choose it. Going where you want means feeling discomfort, maybe not this cold shower's, so practice feeling it, get used to it, and make it your friend, because discomfort is your friend
- These seem so powerful and are encouraged here, but whether or not you agree with them, suggested: step back today and think about what foundational things carry you. Foundations are not complex: simple, right things, done every day. That is the only way they provide a foundation to grow from: done once a month, they are worthless
- If you don't know, decide: these are the things, starting today. Or look back at what you have and haven't been doing. We know when we're off the path: putting a foundation in place is such a powerful shift, and you'll go where you want and feel better. If parts are missing, put those habits in place, across your physical and mental space
- The discipline to do these few simple things every day makes you feel better the whole rest of the day. Put in the effort to exercise for 20 minutes or 10, half an hour or an hour, and you feel good the whole rest of the 24 hours; skip it, and "I know I should work out" gnaws at you the whole 24 hours. With that discipline we get to build and move forward, and it's more fun
- If you haven't gotten after it yet, get down now: some push-ups, go outside, some squats, start with one. Already got a session in? Go get number two in
