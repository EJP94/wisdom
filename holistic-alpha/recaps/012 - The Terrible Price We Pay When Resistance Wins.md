> **TL;DR** — The real price of letting resistance win is regret, felt most when life ends: feel into what you'd regret and act on it today, since working towards a goal is enough to eliminate regret, the target all the techniques serve.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 12, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- We don't always beat **resistance**, and if we don't actively attack it within ourselves, the losses accumulate and we pay a terrible price: regret
- We'll all face the end of this life, slowly over two years or suddenly, a week or sixty years from now. **Story:** that night at the climbing gym, a good friend had an apparent heart attack and left by ambulance for the ER
- Personally, the one thing wanted more than anything is probably to not have regret: it's so painful. We feel it day to day, even within a day, for not having accomplished maybe what we set out to in the first part of the day, or for what we didn't do a week or a month ago. Feel into the regret you'll have on your deathbed, or in that moment with things flashing through your mind
- **Story:** so much of life was lived in a state of resistance; its short-term regret and pain were nothing next to what the end would bring had it continued. The wish now, above all, is to eliminate those sources of regret and live today in a way that eliminates it, and the same for you
- Don't regret not starting the business, not leaving the job or the partner who's not right for you, not asking the one who is right to marry you, not going for that sports achievement, whatever it is. It doesn't take much talking to older people around us to see and feel the pain of regret for things not done
- Being alpha male is really about battling resistance, because the real price is not being overweight, broke, in shitty relationships or lost in stress and your thoughts, those short-term things: it's regret. Resistance wants to stomp on your throat and shove regret in your face. Sit back, let everything be tomorrow, and he beats us and we regret it. End of story: we either do something about it or we don't
- **Story:** years and years doing nothing about it, then years damn well doing something. Whether you start here or are already fighting resistance, keep going or start today: the only way to avoid regret tomorrow is to do the thing today
- Imagine chest pains a week from now, realizing your life may be about to end: what would you really regret? "I'll bet a bunch of them are relationships because the people in our life mean more than anything." Maybe your fitness and health, your mind, because you can't get its things into your physical world, or your kids and being a better father. Feel into it: listening to a podcast like this, chest pains and weakness in your arms, an ambulance 10 minutes later, unconscious 20 minutes later, and live today to not regret that
- You don't have to accomplish the big end goal to not regret, just be working towards it: the moment you shift from "I'm gonna do that someday" to actually doing something is the moment you eliminate regret. You're not going to regret not having maybe accomplished your greatest end goal, but you'll damn well regret not having started. Regret is not some far-off thing: we can eliminate it and build power in our life by doing things today
- Life is short and fragile in some ways and really, really long in others: it might end next week, or 5, 10 or 40 years from now, so how do you want to live that time? What we can change long term is absolutely without limit, but it starts today. Otherwise regret comes day to day, month to month, and at the end. "That's the way it is"
- **Immediate win**: act today on whatever is gnawing at you most and you win immediately against regret and resistance. If tomorrow brings chest pains, or a car accident with just enough time to say goodbye to your family, at least you'll know you made the decision: you ran out of time, but you tried. Put things off and years go by, then a lifetime
- Personally, one mission in life right now: eliminating sources of that kind of regret, highly encouraged as your overall life target too. It means something different for each of us, depending on our life, mind, heart, spirit and who we're meant to be: nobody knows what everybody should do to live the life of their dreams
- It's easy not to be motivated by a positive goal, a weird thing as humans: sometimes avoiding pain motivates us much more than seeking a reward. If the reward hasn't got you to act, flip it around and feel the regret 10 or 40 years on, at the end, having done nothing about what was eating at you. Avoiding that pain is a major motivator, personally, and probably for you too
- That's why we fight resistance, build strength and physical power, train our mind and harness our sex power: to know at the end that we lived boldly, courageously, lovingly, intentionally, without painful regret. That's alpha male: on the gym floor with chest pains, knowing "I went for what I was feeling. I went for what was really me." That's the target all the techniques, nutrition and fitness are for
- If you never listen to another word of this show, go do whatever you're feeling: you already really know those things
