> **TL;DR** — The one change with the most impact is pursuing intentional discomfort: all growth happens through it and it is the gateway to your deepest self, so use even forced discomfort as a tool and choose it, from cold showers to hard physical work.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 80, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- New year goals are awesome, but one focus will have a more dramatic impact on transforming your life than anything else: **intentional discomfort**. Not the gym, a better diet or a particular change in your relationships, business or work. Those are all great, but the one thing is getting uncomfortable
- Two things are true about getting uncomfortable:
  - All growth happens through it. You do not grow when things feel nice and safe and comfortable. A new way of eating, a new way of communicating with your spouse, putting in the physical work, voicing your opinion at work: each is uncomfortable, at least at the start, and that is where growth happens. Think more fundamentally than your body, weight or job goals: we change by committing to being uncomfortable, "I am willing to go out today in every aspect of my life and step into discomfort"
  - It is where you access your deepest self: your gateway to the real you, in that quiet space that's powerful beyond explanation. Physical effort, cold exposure, stepping up socially in a way you haven't before: discomfort forces you to a deeper, more quiet, central, powerful place
- If you never listen to another word of this podcast, listen to this: pursue discomfort and you will grow, and experience the version of you and the life you want to experience
- Discomfort that feels sort of forced upon you, a divorce, a job change, is the same: an opportunity to use it as a tool to go deeper within yourself and grow. Whether you choose it or it's happening to you, discomfort is where it's at
- To make 2020 the best year of your life, one thing will surely get you there more than anything else: put yourself in uncomfortable places, do uncomfortable things:
  - If it's cold where you wake up, walk outside: it's not about being warm
  - Start your day with a cold shower. Beyond the many benefits of cold therapy for your body, it's choosing discomfort, saying at the start of the day that you are willing to get uncomfortable because that leads where you want to go and uncovers the version of you that you can feel inside. **Story:** exclusively cold showers for the last four or five years
  - Work physically hard
  - Be willing to get uncomfortable with a change of diet, a social interaction, a thing at work, interactions with your spouse. Don't resist that, pursue it
- Pursue discomfort and you will transform your life and your life experience more than through any other possible thing, in ways you can only imagine
- **Story:** the singular goal for this year: to look back at its end and say other guys were helped to kick ass, feel good and have a better year than the year before
