> **TL;DR** — Living your truth, not others' opinions, is the only way to your real power: misalignment is driving with one foot on the brake and feeds resistance, so find what's out of line, stop caring what people think, love yourself enough and change it today.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 17, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- **Living your truth**, whatever that means to you, is the only way to access your real abilities, willpower, strength and creativity. We can't live somebody else's truth: we don't even know it, though too often we guess it
- Too often we base so much of our choices on what someone else thinks, or what we think they'll think: a spouse, parents, kids, friends, coworkers. That path leads not just to less happiness but to less power
- It's like driving with your foot on the gas and the brake at the same time: basically nowhere, on a real rough ride. Living what's true for you individually is the only way to get your foot off the brake; on the gas, looking ahead, you make a hell of a lot more progress
- An encouragement for today: think about what in your life is maybe not in alignment with who you truly are, a job, a relationship your heart isn't in anymore, personal habits and lifestyles not really in alignment with who you truly know you are, friends with a negative impact, and how it's impacting you. Maybe you already know deep down. The moment you align it with who you are and what you desire, your power and speed towards the life you want are truly unleashed; while it stays out of line, you're never going to access your real power
- Out of alignment, **resistance** within us gains incredible power over us. Brave enough to change and live in a way that feels true and right to you, regardless of what anybody thinks, you flip the tables and gain incredible power over him
- Too often we give too many fucks away to other people. Stop giving a fuck what people think: what do you think, who are you, what do you really want? With one life to live, that's what matters
- Beyond happiness, your ability to create, build, grow and progress is directly linked with your alignment with who you truly are: the more you discover and follow it, the greater the power
- Don't wait for the power, mood or strength to make the change: make it, and your power will grow exponentially
- Love is what it's all about: not about not loving others, but about loving yourself enough to do what you know is right for you
- Make some kind of decision today: everybody can live more in alignment, more of who we are and less of who we think somebody wants us to be. Be what you really want, without waiting for someone to say it's okay, and "you will kick more ass than you can possibly imagine"
