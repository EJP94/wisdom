> **TL;DR** — Stopping porn, the NoFap goal, is worthy but only like step two of 26; the Holistic Alpha way also builds sexuality into a source of energy, confidence and creation, and four indispensable tools help overcome porn: purpose, meditation, cold exposure and a plan.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 540, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- The show is about kicking ass as men from the inside out, to create the life and relationships we desire and uncover our most powerful version. What sets this movement apart is the focus on the power of our sexuality: it impacts every aspect of life, so we absolutely need to dial it in, through the lens of empowered, sacred, healed sexuality
- **NoFap.** Technically a community, a forum. In its own description: "NoFap is a community-based peer support website to help people overcome porn addiction and other forms of compulsive sexual behavior". What a lot of guys understand by it: stopping the porn-masturbation-ejaculation habit. A very worthy goal, but very incomplete
  - Imagine being asked before coming to Earth, depending on what you believe, what you want out of your life as a sexual being. Would you say "I want to stop my compulsive sexual behavior"? "I doubt it. I very much doubt it"
  - Stopping porn and compulsive behavior is only a very small part of healing our sexuality and getting everything out of it. In the long-term journey, getting past porn is "actually like step two out of 26" (a foreign concept to some, clear to those further along): not an easy step, and it needs to happen, but a very small part of the process
- **The Holistic Alpha way** approaches sexuality knowing that, for better or worse, it will have a big impact on our life. Not only heal the negative aspects and get rid of porn and compulsive behavior, but work on all the positive aspects: turn sexuality and sexual practices into physical energy and confidence, improved sexual experiences with partners, and sexual energy transmuted into creativity, life force and confidence. A whole world of positive impacts, as an individual and in a relationship: expanding sexuality, improving our access to it and harnessing it into life, new versions of ourselves, things we create, people we attract and relationships we build
- **Four indispensable tools**, in his mind, for overcoming porn for good, which also help in this entire process:
  - **Purpose**, a real reason why. Guys don't really care about stopping porn itself. Maybe it's affecting your erections with your girlfriend or wife; maybe you feel the energetic drain and know you won't be disciplined enough to create the life you want; maybe you want to reconnect with your soul after the disconnection and inner-world effect of porn; maybe a combination, or something completely different. A reason of only "porn sucks", it drags me down, is okay, but not really enough: not doing something is not sufficient motivation for the vast majority of human beings. We need a target. Encouraged: don't leave it general and vague. Play out the two scenarios, what positive things will or won't happen depending on whether you get past porn, and the positive scenario of what you really want is your why
  - **Meditation**, said a lot on the show for a very good reason. Overcoming porn, and actually the vast majority of this sexual health stuff, has some physical aspects, practices and techniques, but a big part of the journey of our sexual health, and certainly overcoming porn, is an inner world game: your mind and emotions drive it one way or the other, and you'll need a new and improved handle on them. "there is no more fundamental way to get a better handle on what is happening in our mind and our emotions than meditation, period": the fundamental inner-world self-care practice
    - Biking or walking may be meditative, and that's great, but a sitting-still meditation, just with your inner world, has certain value
    - Bigger picture, it helps you understand what is going on in your inner world, including some of your triggers and how to work through them, and become more of an observer instead of getting caught up
    - In the moment of the urge toward the compulsive behavior you no longer want, your ability to choose a new path depends on how you approach that brief window of opportunity in your inner world. Meditation is the foundational way to upgrade how you respond in those moments. The vast majority of things affecting our mental, physical, emotional and sexual health (porn, working out, a conversation with your wife, how you eat) are driven by very brief windows of opportunity, and moving through them well depends on your connection with your inner world
    - If you're not meditating, please start. A great way is the Headspace app: look for Headspace basics. Insight Timer is another; there are a ton of ways, so find one that works for you. If it didn't really stick before, or you haven't ever really tried, Headspace is very much recommended; if you have another way, go for it
  - **Cold exposure**: a cold shower, a cold plunge, even time outside, like a walk or hike. Some of what it does in body and mind is very directly linked with getting past porn:
    - Dopamine. As you may know from the NoFap community or forums, porn messes up your dopamine system in major ways, so we need to heal it toward a more stable, reliable supply and response, without giant artificial spikes and giant crashes ("a very simplistic way to describe it"). As little as 11 minutes of cold exposure per week, not per day, "has shown significant benefits" in dopamine levels and response, because boosting dopamine is one of the body's immediate responses to cold, in a very natural, healthy way, raised for hours afterward
    - Dopamine is absolutely not a bad thing. You might associate it with pleasure, but it is known as the motivation molecule. What matters is where we get it from and how stable our levels are. On the porn-masturbation-ejaculation cycle you get a massive spike, then crash, and basically run most of your life on super low levels. We want healthy, steady levels all the time, which help us be motivated, disciplined, feel good and get shit done
    - Norepinephrine and epinephrine go up, basically adrenaline, what gives you energy: the instinct to freak out, move, maybe yell in the cold is those neurotransmitters at work. Like dopamine, they stay raised for hours. With more mental energy through your day, in moments of urges, workout and food choices, you make better choices and get better outcomes
    - Grit and toughness. You'll have to do tough shit to create the life you want, "Period". Overcoming porn will be hard, with hard moments to push through; so will the body, business and relationships you want. Stop hoping for it not to be hard and get better at doing hard things: regular cold exposure is regular practice in doing hard things
    - Other benefits not directly about porn: it boosts your immune system and reduces inflammation, among various others
    - Personally, so many guys, coaching clients and guys from training programs, have said over and over that the cold shower became a staple once they started. It is also probably the most effective emotional reset personally, "or at least one of them": very powerful when you're feeling down, stressed, depressed or lethargic
    - Dose: in essence, about 11 minutes per week is a sweet spot, about a minute and a half per day or so; no need to push significantly beyond it or set records. If the water is not as cold (Florida cold showers are not comparatively all that cold), you may want to push up a little, a three-, four- or five-minute cold shower. In very, very cold water you can be on the shorter end
    - Now that it's winter in a lot of places, a walk or hike in minimal clothes works too. **Story:** walks and jogs in the Utah winter in just shorts, no shirt and barefoot (barefoot running). It takes longer, because water pulls heat out of your body much faster than air: two, three or five minutes outside is refreshing but doesn't do what the same time in cold water does
  - **A plan.** If your only plan is what you're not going to do, "you're screwed". On the coast of California, with the entire rest of the United States to your east, answering where you're going with "not going into the ocean" tells you nothing. If your only plan is to stop masturbating and looking at porn, it won't get you where you want to go, "I promise". You need an actual plan of where to go
- The aim of this work is not only to move past the compulsive, unwanted stuff that drags you down but, as or more importantly, to move deeply into the positive end, where sexuality becomes deeply and powerfully empowering: lifting you up, brightening your energy, improving your relationships, creativity, discipline and ability to create. **Story:** still on the journey, with no end point, always learning and growing, yet sexuality in his life now versus several years ago, and certainly 10 or 15 years ago, is "light years different"
- In a community, surrounded by other guys working on this, with similar priorities and experiences, you make progress way faster
