> **TL;DR** — A very small number of trickle-down habits, good or bad, affect so many other things in your day, like cold showers, porn, your last hours before bed, exercise and journaling; dial in the most important few and the rest unfolds with much less effort.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 422, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Listening means you're on the journey to get better, probably not 100% of the time: we all have setbacks. Not everyone is really on it intentionally and actively. It affects other people too: set a burning log next to one that isn't and the other catches fire, and the same happens with human beings
- **Story:** after a morning training session, cold showers came up with a few friends; a couple don't take them and asked why. The answer: a lot of physical benefits; personally the biggest direct impact is mental, on mind and emotions all day. But the biggest benefit, a key reason it has long been an all-the-time thing, is that personally it is a trickle-down habit
- **Trickle-down habits.** The little things you do most or all days (personal care, family life, eating, driving) add up to a pretty large number. Some habits obviously have more direct impact, but more than that, they differ in how they trigger other habits
- **80/20.** The Pareto principle, or 80/20 rule, basically says 20% of your actions deliver 80% of the results, so the other 80% only deliver 20%: a lot of what we do doesn't move the needle that much. It applies to everything from cheese from cows in Wisconsin to businesses to economies, and certainly to our personal life. And there's another level: apply 80/20 again to that 20%, and 20% of it again gives 80% of the results, layer by layer
- So a very small number of habits trigger or trickle down to lots of other things occurring or not: a very small number of levers, working both ways equally, positive and negative. A huge part of improving, especially for lasting and meaningful transformations, is identifying and dialing in your trickle-down habits. You very likely have at least a couple at work now, maybe unaware; you may have a positive one, two, three or four that you know are trickle-down habits, leading to other good things
- **Story:** once a cold shower is knocked out, the day unfolds differently, known from testing it; the difference is so huge that it's been a cold shower every single day for years. There are tons of examples, some quite common with a lot of guys and humans, some particular to you
- Negative side: porn. One reason it is so devastating for guys often is that it is a major negative trickle-down habit, changing downstream things for hours, even days. Often: you look at porn, masturbate, ejaculate, are filled with shame and wish you hadn't done it; or maybe not filled with shame, maybe not even aware of it yet, but you feel different, energy and confidence dragging. Next morning you get up later and skip the workout; next afternoon you're kind of down and have that beer; a couple of beers mean bed later and waking later. It plays out in so many ways, including with your girlfriend, wife or partner
- To really grow, first identify negative trickle-down habits at play. Maybe going to bed, common with quite a few people: our night has a huge impact on the next day. For a lot of people, some of them are the last hour or two before bed and what time you go to bed. Off screens for two hours before bed, reading, stretching, unwinding, in bed at a good time, or on screens until late? The next-day difference in how you feel and act is in all likelihood dramatic
- **Physical exercise**, good for most, if not all, people, is a huge trickle-down habit: more energy, different actions at work, better sleep, more happiness, dropping that weight, a body that feels better
- For most of us negative ones are at work, though it kind of depends on how much you've optimized; we all can still do better. Limiting, eliminating or changing probably one or two would kind of automatically make the day unfold better
- We can also add positive ones. **Journaling** is a great example: some people journal, not a lot, and it is hugely valuable for understanding our inner world. More awareness and clarity about your thoughts, past and visions for the future mean more and better action and generally relating to your world better
- Which trickle-down habits affect you now, and which are the biggest to add? "I guarantee you have both." A very common thing in coaching: find the key levers, the couple of things to change and to add so the days and weeks unfold differently. Dial in your specifics through awareness and practice, pull the right few levers, and things change in major ways
- Most importantly, maybe, things change with less effort and less grinding [?], more automatically, as a natural next step. Changing your mental, physical or sexual health, or some aspect of your life, a lot of times feels like forcing so many changes. In reality, if through awareness, understanding and intention you dial in the most important few things, the rest unfolds with much less effort than you'd think
