> **TL;DR** — A guided deep relaxation lying on your back: breaths, body weight and senses, a head-to-toe scan, counted breaths, relaxing from the toes up, then three visualizations (limp spaghetti, marionette, cement), about 10 minutes, with even greater benefits done longer on your own.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 252, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- **Guided practice:** deep relaxation, about 10 minutes
  - The best results come lying down: lie flat on your back, comfortably, where you won't be physically disturbed for the next, say, 10 minutes or so
  - Eyes open, take a few deep breaths in through the nose and out through the mouth, letting a little of the tension, of that energy inside you, go on each out-breath
  - On the next exhale, close your eyes and mouth, breathe through the nose, and allow your body to breathe
  - Feel the weight of your body on the ground: maybe your back, shoulders, back of the hips, heels, hands, arms, head. Let your awareness settle there for a moment
  - Let the sounds come and go, with no need to focus on them; be aware of maybe the smells, the air against your skin, and settle into those sensations
  - Over the next, say, 15 or 20 seconds, gently and smoothly scan from the top of your head down to your toes, changing nothing, aware of how your body feels
  - Rest on your breath, one inhale, one exhale at a time, and count to 10, one on the inhale and two on the exhale. It's okay if your mind wanders: come back to the breath, and start counting over if you'd like, or continue where you left off
  - From your toes and feet, step by step, intentionally let go of a little bit of your body at a time: feet, ankles, maybe calves, hips, back, chest and shoulders, face and jaw, letting face, scalp and jaw sink towards the floor
  - Shift to the power of the mind, of visualization, to go deeper: imagine floating above yourself, looking down at your body lying there, your limbs loose, limp, cooked spaghetti noodles, with no desire to move, just resting and settling. See and feel it as much as you can
  - A couple of breaths with your awareness back on the breath
  - Picture yourself as a marionette doll: your elbows, wrists, knees and hips aren't solid, just connected with loose, limp string. Maybe somebody picks up your leg and it just falls back to the floor. Feel yourself sinking deeper into the floor, towards the earth
  - A couple more nice breaths
  - Final visualization: your arms and legs encased in cement, so heavy they sink deeper and deeper into the mattress, on your bed, really or imagined. Maybe somebody walks in, tries to pick up your leg and can't, it's too heavy
  - Finish with your awareness on your breath for the next 30 seconds or so, sinking into the relaxation you made space for; if your mind wanders, that's okay, bring it back
  - Gently become aware of your surroundings again, the sounds, maybe the air on your skin. Whenever you're ready, open your eyes and, as much as possible, let the physical relaxation continue: a soft, seamless transition into the rest of your day
- This exercise takes about 10 minutes or so. Done on your own for much longer, 20, 30 or 40 minutes with the same steps and visualizations, it brings "incredible benefits", even greater the longer you do it
