> **TL;DR** — Resistance, an inner opposing force that never dies, is beaten not area by area but by strengthening who you are from the inside out, which seems to come down to your physical body and hormones, fasting, sexual energy and your mind.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 1, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- The universe is governed by a law of duality, of opposing forces, everywhere and in everything: opposite the light is dark. They don't cancel each other out but balance each other out, within us and our lives too
- We have a drive, a power that wants to carry us towards our dreams and who we're supposed to be. Opposite that good inside you is an opposing force, **resistance**, as it's called here:
  - Not other people, not anything in your physical world: it lives inside you, living and breathing every day as much as you are
  - Its one purpose is to push back: to hold you back from where you want to go and who you want to be, to make you stop instead of putting in the effort, to keep you quiet when you should speak up in a relationship
  - It will be with you every day, in every aspect of your life. You cannot kill it: resistance never dies
- Resistance seems to show up in every single aspect of life, so beating it is not about those individual aspects but about stepping back and changing, evaluating and strengthening who you are as a man from the inside out
- Get stronger in all the ways resistance can't handle, so you face it every day from strength, as "this big, confident, badass", and it comes cowering
- You probably have areas, as is personally the case too, where you're "really kicking his ass", and others where it hands you yours. The more areas you beat it in, the more it gets the message, and the easier it gets
- The podcast is about one thing: unleashing your true power by strengthening the man you are, so you battle resistance everywhere from strength
- Beating resistance seems to come down to a few things:
  - Physical well-being is absolutely critical: your body affects your mind, emotions, relationships and success in business. You can't attack resistance from your greatest strength without taking care of it, and that starts with fitness, nutrition, the right supplements and optimizing your hormones. When resistance beats you physically, it's like the first battle of the day is already lost, and you go into every other one weaker
  - Hormones are super important. As they say, "hormones affect everything and everything affects your hormones", and it's so true: how you feel, perceive, act and think, your confidence, your mental and physical well-being. Optimizing them and your testosterone, an ongoing topic, puts you in the strongest position to beat resistance and get what you desire
  - Fasting, not a daily topic, is such a powerful tool against resistance, like turning on a "temporary superpower against resistance"
  - Sexual energy, or sex power, is not just critical in your sex life: it affects your life force energy, drive, confidence, physical energy, joy and happiness, every part of you. Built over time, it carries you to incredible places. Single, married or divorced, there are always ways to harness it, or you can waste it, which drains you physically, of life force energy and against resistance. At the fundamental level it seems to impact literally how strong you are against resistance: the more you build it, the stronger you are, so managing it is amazingly important
  - The mind. However strong your body and sexual power, if you don't understand your mind and aren't willing to work with it positively, none of the rest matters. It doesn't take a lot: attitude and mindset, working out in a way that builds the mind's strength for tough situations, and a willingness to sit still and meditate for a few minutes
- None of this is honestly complicated, but that doesn't mean it's easy. Get after each area every day
- Stop comparing your body, business or relationships to anybody else's. Focus on being better today than yesterday, personally the whole aim, and the podcast's goal for you
- We're not wolves, we're human beings. Being an alpha male is not about dominating other people but about dominating resistance within you
