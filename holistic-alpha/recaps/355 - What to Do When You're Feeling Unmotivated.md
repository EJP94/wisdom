> **TL;DR** — Feeling unmotivated, two suggestions: take a cold shower, an incredible reset, and chunk it down to one very small action, like five push-ups, because a body at rest tends to stay at rest and a body in motion tends to stay in motion.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 355, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- **Story:** a 13-year-old young man transforming around his sexual health and porn gave an update: two weeks without porn and without ejaculating, with some edging along the way, feeling better, more confident and stronger than ever before. His max push-ups went from 35 to 40 and his strict pull-ups from two to five
- That is proof there's no excuse for any of us to not be kicking ass: at 13 he is doing things, and figuring out things, that personally came many, many years later. Guys of all ages and backgrounds, seen personally, make incredible transformations. If you haven't really been moving forward the way you kind of know you should, keep taking steps: "that's the only way you climb a big mountain is one step at a time"
- **Story:** thinking ahead to summer days not really hanging out with friends and without necessarily a lot to do, the young man was feeling kind of unmotivated and asked what to do
- There are probably a number of ways to address it, and some may work best for you. A couple of suggestions:
  - **Cold shower.** So powerful for shifting your mind, body and spirit: an incredible reset button, with a boost of basically feel-good neurotransmitters, like norepinephrine, that lasts for hours. Very simple, nothing earth-shattering. **Story:** over the last five years, the number of days feeling kind of blah in the morning until the cold shower, and then everything changed, is incredible. Guys say it over and over: in Bootcamp training, run a couple of times in the last year or two, the cold shower habit was one of the things that stuck most with a number of guys, for the difference it makes physically, mentally, emotionally. It is one of the best ways to sort of combat a downer, depression kind of feeling
  - **Chunk it down** and do something very, very small: the most powerful thing found personally, and what is recommended to people who aren't feeling motivated. Unmotivated, we're at rest, and by one of the sort of fundamental laws of our physical world, a body at rest tends to stay at rest and a body in motion tends to stay in motion. Go for a five-minute walk or do five push-ups; with a bunch of sales calls, make one. A physical thing (five squats, say) seems great because it gets you moving
- One small productive step makes you a body in motion. You may not feel super motivated after five push-ups, but you'll be moving and a little more motivated to do something else. Stuck, overwhelmed, or not feeling like that workout, do something small in a positive direction. You'll likely find it cracks the door open into a different state of being, and one productive thing probably leads to another
- **Story:** days stuck in that unmotivated place, getting not much if anything done, especially in the earlier years, days and months strung together that were pretty much all like that. In personal experience and with guys on coaching calls, doing one thing shifts your state of being and of mind
- We're all going to have unmotivated days. Habits and systems are a big part of moving through them: a daily, automatic habit helps carry you. Even so, some days will still be hard to start
- If even the idea of a cold shower feels uncomfortable, do five push-ups: then you're more likely to hit that cold shower, and after both, to move into whatever comes next
