> **TL;DR** — Beside Emerson's one mind common to all men there is one fire, one unstoppable life force you are an inlet to, so you do not battle resistance alone; honor yourself and your fellow man by recognizing it within, doing the work and looking within.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 79, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- **Quote:** Ralph Waldo Emerson: "There is one mind common to all individual men. Every man is an inlet to the same and to all of the same."
- There is also one fire common to all individual men, one indomitable spirit. Just as surely as we are part of the one mind Emerson refers to, we are part of one unstoppable life force, one strength. You are an inlet to the full power of this force
- As you battle resistance to persist through challenges, you do not battle alone: "We battle as one." We are brothers, forever connected by and fueled by the same power
- Honor yourself and your fellow man by recognizing this force within yourself. Do the work. Look within. The spirit and strength we all share flows through you
