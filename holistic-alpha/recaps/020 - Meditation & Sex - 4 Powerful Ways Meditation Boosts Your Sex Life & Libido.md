> **TL;DR** — Meditation boosts sex drive and sex life four ways: it destroys stress, raises hormones like DHEA, by as much as 40% in meditators, and testosterone downstream, releases endorphins much as running does, and brings a present, quiet mind, making you simply a better lover.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 20, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Meditation has a direct and powerful link not only to your physical state and sex drive, but also to your performance and enjoyment of sex itself. Four ways:
  - **Stress**: meditation destroys stress, and stress destroys sex drive
    - Stress puts the body into fight or flight mode, where things like sex drive are basically shut down and take a back seat to surviving: the state cavemen were in defending against an animal. Today it's work, relationships, whatever stress weighs on us. The more stress, the more sex drive feels the effects
    - Meditation is without question one of the most powerful, if not the most powerful, ways to reduce stress, with countless studies and science on its effects on cortisol and stress hormones, so much proven data
    - To the extent we reduce stress, we improve sex drive: it's just the way our bodies work, a direct inverse relationship. Less stress, and the more you feel like having sex, the better you focus, the more present you are, the better you perform and the more you enjoy it. Better for life, and certainly for your sex life
  - **Hormones**: tremendous physical benefits on a hormonal level. Less stress means less cortisol and related stress hormones, and meditation also improves the hormones we want
    - One really important one is DHEA, also referred to as kind of the grandmother hormone. It acts on its own, but also produces lots of downstream hormones, including testosterone for men and estrogen for women
    - DHEA is really closely linked to aging: levels gradually decline as we age, and doctors use it as one of the markers of sort of the true age of your body, versus your birth certificate. Higher DHEA, more youth
    - Meditators were studied and found to have higher DHEA levels than non-meditators by as much as 40%: a huge, drastic impact on actual hormone levels. It's not just about a clear mind, concentration or presence: there are really dramatic physical benefits to mind and body
    - More DHEA not only increases youth, it also tends to increase sex drive, because it's converted into downstream hormones like testosterone
  - **Endorphins**: a lot of people don't really associate them with meditation
    - A lot of people know endorphins are released with exercise, as in a runner's high, and make us feel incredibly good. They also lead to a lot of sort of mind and physical state conducive to a positive sex drive and sex experience
    - Meditation boosts them too: one study in the Biological Psychology Journal in 1995, "so it's been a while", studied elite runners and meditators and found very similar endorphin-boosting effects
    - They make you feel awesome, put you more in the mood and more able to enjoy it, with really powerful physiological effects on your body and mental and physical state
  - **Presence**: without question, meditation helps your ability to be present, with a focused, quiet mind
    - One of the other big things that kills sex drive, and especially your sex life and ability to perform and enjoy, is your ability to be present. If you're mentally distracted and your mind is always somewhere else, you're never going to be in the moment anywhere near the same way. It affects performance and enjoyment, and when constant throughout the day, your sex drive too
    - The more you get comfortable with your mind and step back, not caught up in all the clouds running around but chilling in the blue sky above them, the more you can be in the moment with sex, your sex energy and your sex life
    - With a more focused and quiet mind you're simply a better lover: you last longer, you're more attentive, you enjoy sex better and you're more able to control your arousal level
- Meditation has really powerful benefits in so many aspects of life, and one of them is most definitely your sex life
