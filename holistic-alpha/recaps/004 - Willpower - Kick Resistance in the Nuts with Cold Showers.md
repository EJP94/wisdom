> **TL;DR** — A cold shower every day, the earlier the better, lowers stress, raises alertness and immune response, and above all builds willpower against resistance, while warm showers personally left a drained feeling: the cold is your warm friend.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 4, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Cold is amazing and beneficial on so many levels. There's a lot of science on exposing your body to cold and getting used to it, including incredible immune responses, but personally, the biggest thing found is that it takes you to a deeper, central, more powerful place
- Cold is one of the most powerful ways to "kick resistance in the nuts". Society raised us used to being warm and comfortable, and getting uncomfortable through cold has unbelievable benefits against resistance
- The encouragement: start one practice, **cold showers**. **Story:** after cold showers came ice baths, and generally a tank top all winter, seeking the cold whenever possible. There are lots of ways to do it
- It's got to be every day: twice a week just doesn't do the same thing. Stick with it a little and, with absolute certainty, you'll feel really amazing benefits
- How:
  - The earlier in the day, the better
  - If you have never taken a cold shower at all, you can start by finishing on cold, then move to just cold
  - Otherwise, straight cold, maybe with a little warm water to wash, and practice it as much as you can. You can do it a lot more than you think: it takes intentionality and a decision. Gradually build how long you stay in
- Benefits:
  - Lower stress. The cold stresses your body a little in the moment and, as with lifting weights, body and mind adapt and grow stronger, a huge part being your mind's ability to stay in the moment and not be swept away or crushed by stress
  - Alertness, not just for the 30 seconds or five or 10 minutes in there but all day. There's really a lot of evidence now, from science and from countless people incorporating cold. It also stimulates deeper breaths, which alone has a lasting effect
  - Immune response: there are actually studies that found cold showers increase your white blood cells, essentially your body's immune system
  - The biggest: you literally increase your willpower, your strength against resistance in every area of life
- **Story:** strictly cold showers for between two and three years now. Warm showers used to feel good temporarily, then leave a drained and tired feeling
- Things that feel good temporarily but leave you worse off suck, like alcohol. Personally, and probably for a lot of people, if you really feel into it, a long, hot shower is that: it feels good, then a lot of times leaves you more drained
- Starting your day in a cold shower immediately kicks resistance right in the nuts: not for comfort, but as an intentional choice, for the benefits, strength and internal power it brings
- If you've never taken one, it's hard to see cold as anything but discomfort. It is a little, but like working out, it's temporary discomfort that leads to much more powerful things
- If you're doing nothing with cold yet, you don't need ice baths or outside cold exposure: please just try a cold shower. They "seriously kick ass" and will change your life. **Story:** after the shift to cold showers, there was never any going back, in years, for the difference they made in life and in the mind
- The cold is absolutely your warm friend: inside, where your internal fire, power, willpower and the real you are, is fueled and supported by the cold
