> **TL;DR** — Rather than facing the whole project, use the day's 96 15-minute blocks, where the change lies: set a timer and give 15 minutes daily to the most important thing, or to not doing a negative one, in relationships, sleep, nutrition, business, fitness and mornings.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 19, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Every 24-hour period holds 96 15-minute blocks, and 1/96th of the day can change your life, and is changing it, for better or for worse. Too often we look at the big, huge task and everything it will take, and let that stop us from doing the thing: repairing a relationship, getting fit, eating better, building a business, being a better father. Mentally trying to reach the place of having accomplished the whole thing makes it seem huge
- That's not the best way to look at it, and not how it works: we live life a moment at a time, really, not a project at a time. But we need some sort of period we can attack and really make changes in: 15 minutes, or thereabouts
- In 15 minutes you might work your body (every day, and you're way more fit), drive to the liquor store for beer, have real communication with your spouse, or get lost on social media. The really good, powerful things can be knocked out 15 minutes at a time, at least, and what negatively affects us is those short periods too, sometimes the ones that don't happen, like 15 minutes of real communication with your spouse every day. That's where all of the change lies, where your power lies
- A strong belief in being present, and in meditating to practice presence, since that seems to be where we're most effective and also most joyful: a moment-by-moment thing. But planning or eliminating an activity, creating or eliminating a habit, takes a little more than a moment. "15 minutes you can change your life. 15 minutes you are changing your life"
- Jerking off daily probably takes less than 15 minutes; to the point of ejaculation, in kind of a negative way where you lose that energy, almost guaranteed probably less. Set a timer: your only job for those 15 minutes is to eliminate it. Day after day, your internal power and fire completely change. You don't have to do it all at once
- **Set a timer**: if your relationship with your spouse could use improving, set one in the other room, and when it goes off, immediately walk over and talk for 15 minutes, not forcing conversation if it's not the right time. It doesn't have to be six hours, or the whole project, or getting all the way fit, building the whole business or fixing the whole relationship at once. 15 minutes at a time, every day, will change your life in more powerful ways than you can possibly imagine. For a relationship, the most important thing is probably communicating, planned every day: call that person, or talk to your spouse about what has kind of come up in the last few days, or "I've been feeling this, or I see maybe you're feeling that". Real communication will do amazing things
- "We are the things that we do every day." It doesn't take a lot of time, but it takes an intention. A negative activity probably happens at the same time every day: when it's about to start, set a timer and just don't do it for 15 minutes
- Sleep: if you don't sleep well, maybe set a timer 15 minutes before bed and wind down in the most effective ways you can figure out, learn and grow with over time
- Nutrition: maybe 15 minutes at the start of the day in the kitchen, seeing what you have and strategizing the fuel that will fuel you effectively and what to buy at the store. Decide in your mind how you'll fuel your body today and more often than not you'll follow through, if not every single time then a pretty good chunk of the time, and change your life and your body. It might take only 45 seconds, but at first maybe you do need the timer, with pen and paper if that's how you roll, or your phone. Strategizing instead of winging it throughout the day gets dramatically different results
- Business: you don't have to build it all at once. Determine the daily activities that make it happen and do them for 15 minutes. If that's all the time you have, or you're not feeling it, fine: 15 minutes every day of whatever feels like the most important thing for that area of your life
- Fitness: if you're not in the shape you want, it does not take a lot of time. Set a timer for 15 minutes and go, every day, and you will see changes, without a doubt
  - The number one thing that matters more than anything else for your fitness is intensity: there are lots of ways to feel better and lots of physical tools, but above all else, just get intense
  - Don't make it a big thing of "intense workouts all the time forever". 15 minutes, set a timer, go as hard as you can: "That's your only job forever." The next day, the same again, at home, at the gym, wherever
- It doesn't take a lot: we have to break stuff into smaller chunks so we can take action, both to add awesomeness to your life and to get rid of things
- Mornings: rolling over onto your phone in bed, checking email and all this stuff first thing, kind of sucks and does crappy things to our brain. Right after we wake up our minds are really open and receptive, so the start of your day guides your mind for the day
  - If your days aren't going the way you want, look at what you do the first 15 minutes, for the next three weeks, to get that habit in place: on waking, set a 15-minute timer on your phone labeled something like "15 minutes of focus", and it clicks you into how-can-I-start-my-day-effectively mode
  - Maybe meditate for five of those minutes, do push-ups for a couple, take a cold shower for a couple, or roll over and love on your spouse for a few: however you can ideally design your day, in your unique way
- Focus on something for 15 minutes. "It's 1% of the day." Everything will change
