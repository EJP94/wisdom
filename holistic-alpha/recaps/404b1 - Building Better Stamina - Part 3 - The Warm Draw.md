- The **warm draw** is somewhat similar to the pressure release valve technique in some ways, and kind of the opposite in others. With the pressure release valve, at, say, 70, 80% arousal, 7 or 8 out of 10, you fully relax mind, body, breathing and root muscle and exhale the pressure out, maybe like steam escaping into the air. - Pressure release valve versus warm draw:
