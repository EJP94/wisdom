> **TL;DR** — Sex can be a great motivator, and since exercise undeniably improves erections, think "earn your erections" in the small moments of decision to train more often and harder, while older or hard-training guys watch for overtraining.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 812, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Sex can be a great motivator: something with a very positive effect on erection quality, stamina or sexual experience is one of the most powerful motivators found, personally and with clients; a very negative effect can likewise be a very powerful motivator to stay away from it. **Story:** used over and over and over again.
- Personally, optimizing in a lot of ways for what empowers the sexual experience, since, in a lot of ways, "when we thrive in our sex life, we thrive in our life", so it can be a guidepost.
- Undeniably, "there is limitless really scientific data", a bunch of studies, showing beyond a shadow of a doubt that exercise improves erection quality, whatever your age, diet or lifestyle: exercise with impact that amps you up, turns on hormones, changes metabolism and neurochemistry. That's aerobic, strength and pelvic floor training. Beyond testosterone, hormones and well-being, exercise improves erections directly, period.
- **Earn your erections**: in a lot of ways we earn our erection quality, or don't really, through how we live; training consistently, sweating, working hard earns it. When you wonder about today's workout, or want to ease up mid-workout, think about earning your erections.
- Probably, as personally and for many other men, erection quality is a very big motivator: use it for a very critical habit, exercise, which improves erections, sexual experience and life.
- Life changes one little moment at a time, in five-second decisions: workout or couch, this food or that. In a lot of ways these brief moments determine the course of our life; use sex as a motivator to decide better in them.
- You have probably experienced this; if not, test it: ramp up your workouts.
- More is not always better, especially as we get older: overtraining is a thing. Frankly, this won't apply to a lot of guys: most need to train harder and more often. But if you've trained a long time and push consistently, watch for overtraining: it can go too far hormonally and hurt erections, hormones, cortisol and more. For most, thinking about earning your erections can be a very simple way to train more often and intensely.
