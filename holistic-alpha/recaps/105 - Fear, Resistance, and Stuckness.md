> **TL;DR** — Collective fear makes resistance strong and leaves us stuck, so fight it daily from both ends of the spectrum: cold showers, 10 minutes of meditation with Headspace, intense eyes-closed push-ups and squats, plus semen retention, the ultimate power against resistance.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 105, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- About a week into most of the world being quarantined, there couldn't be a more important time to unleash your true power as a man. **Resistance** is something we battle in every aspect of life, all the time: in different forms within us, it stands in the way of becoming who we want to be, accomplishing what we want and feeling the way we want. One of its greatest tools against us is fear
- Right now there's so much collective fear energy in the world. You may feel it or not, but it's out there, and fear and uncertainty are such a weapon for resistance, which makes us feel stuck, popping up as fear, doubt, pain, negative thought patterns. It's in a really strong position now: things have changed directly for so many people, and for us all in a lot of ways
- Personally, if this lasted a week or two, maybe chilling to relax and recharge, as all these people say, would do, and that's good. But what if it lasts 18 months? We can't spend 18 months relaxing: we gotta keep progressing. Even once things a little bit go back to normal, a major economic thing means things will take a while to get started again, and for people with businesses things have just changed
- Having talked to so many people, and having really felt it personally: it's really easy right now to feel kind of stuck, physically in our homes for the most part, and mentally. It's good to recharge, definitely good to take time to be really still, but we also need to keep pushing forward
- The tools against resistance fall on one end or the other of a spectrum: getting really, really quiet and still, or getting really uncomfortable and working really hard. Those ends are where we gain the advantage. In the middle we get lost in thought, feel stuck and don't get stuff done
- Number one, a **cold shower**. Every human being is capable of taking one
  - So many physical benefits: the immune system benefits are "actually proven", it boosts your white blood cell count. If you're in good health right now, start; you don't want to necessarily start if you get sick in some way
  - More important than your body: it's like kicking resistance right in the nuts, saying "I will do hard things today", choosing what leads where you want to go even if it's not what feels good or what you want right now
  - If you've never taken one, you can start on warm and kind of transition to cold, but "I would really encourage you", sooner rather than later, over some number of days, to go straight cold. It won't be comfortable, but you can do uncomfortable, hard things, and the payoff in body, mind and spirit is really, really powerful
  - Faced with cold, you either kind of freak out, which you probably will a little if it's new, or go to a deeper place. There's really no in-between: you won't be worrying about the bills or the coronavirus in there. If you somehow can still be lost in thought and stress in a cold shower, you'll be the first person ever talked to who can. Maybe it's possible, but pretty much everything else goes away
  - It takes you into that really calm, quiet, powerful place below thought. Even if that doesn't last a super long time, the effects last throughout your day: you set a precedent for yourself of how the day will go, doing the hard things, focused on the long term
- On the other end of the spectrum, **meditate**. We all have time right now: absolutely no reason all of us cannot be meditating every day. We all got at least 10 minutes back, "no question", from driving around or to work. Finding that still, quiet place is again where we gain power: resistance and stress fade away and the real us comes out
  - It's not instant. Starting can be really intimidating: there's an unknown of what we'll face when we get quiet and watch what's happening inside us, and you might face some hard things initially. If you push through, pretty quickly it becomes a place where resistance, stuckness, angst, anxiety and stress fade away
  - Download Headspace, if you have a phone. Right now it has a really, really awesome 10 day course called Navigating Change, usually part of the paid app, which is so worth it: built on Headspace's core structure, about the idea of change, really perfect right now. Start with 10 minutes a day
- Third, **get uncomfortable, do hard stuff**, because it's hard, for no other reason. Getting uncomfortable outside in the cold, though a lot of us aren't super fortunate to be close to that, or grinding with a workout, which we all can do. It takes intensity: that's where we really gain against resistance
  - A suggestion: figure out how hard you can really go in a 60 to 90 second or two-minute period, combining bodyweight things, which we can all do. **Story:** a shift of focus to bodyweight, since a lot of people connected with on social media don't have a ton of equipment at home, or any: "I think there's no reason we can't kick ass with bodyweight stuff"
  - A suggestion: combine push-ups and squats with your eyes closed, which has such a powerful effect. Go back and forth, whatever your sets look like: three push-ups and five squats, great; 20 push-ups and 50 squats, great; push-ups on your knees, it doesn't matter. When you get burned on one, switch to the other and work as hard as you can. It will not take you a long time
  - Really soon you'll be grinding and faced with a choice: give in to "this is hard", or push through (at some point you have to stop). You can do more than you think, especially with your eyes closed. Keep going even when your body's telling you to stop with that burn, and you really gain ground against resistance
- That ground carries into the rest of life: your work or business, how you're making things work financially right now, your relationships, how calm you are and how well you deal emotionally with all of this. A few simple things win it back
- There are other ways, but everybody can do those three daily: if you have access to water, can sit still and can move your body. **Story:** doing them daily right now: "do it with me". If you've never done them, you will feel the power. A cold shower, your Headspace, then eyes closed, wherever you are, squats and push-ups, every single day; whatever else you want to do is fine
- A fourth: **semen retention**, really the ultimate power gaining back against resistance, the ultimate punching-through force to do what you want and get where you want. Plenty of episodes in the archives cover it. Now could not be a better time to start practicing it
- Fear is resistance's greatest tool, and both are strong right now. We cannot sit idly by, not knowing how long until whatever the new normal is. Resistance keeps us from accomplishing what we want and keeps us feeling kind of shittier than we have to: gain that ground back daily and you'll feel better, even if you already feel great
