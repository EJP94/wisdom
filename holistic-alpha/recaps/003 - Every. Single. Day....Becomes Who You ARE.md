> **TL;DR** — What you do every single day, for weeks and months, becomes who you are, for better or worse, so any change you want probably starts there: make it every day and non-negotiable, not three times a week.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 3, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Any fundamental change seems to start with **every single day**. You are what you do daily, not three times a week, not for two weeks and then stop. What you do every single day for weeks and months literally becomes who you are, not the special events
- So to change, improve, add or even subtract something, it's got to be every day. Any attempt to get in shape, eat better or improve a relationship not focused on every day seems destined to fail
- Conversely, what you do every single day, not twice a week or twice a month, compounds really powerfully. That's how tremendous changes happen
- It works against you as much as for you. **Story:** early in adulthood, absolutely miserable, the daily eating was something like a 7-Eleven hot dog with chili for breakfast and fast food for lunch and on the way home. For a while, almost nightly, literally every single day, came McDonald's and a Taco Bell caramel apple empanada, "this frozen taco thing from hell", with ice cream and caramel syrup added. No Taco Bell in many years now, thank goodness. The result: a disaster zone, physically and mentally
- For the most part we seem to want to add things: habits, routines, powers, skills, knowledge, experiences. So it seems fundamental to look at what you do every single day
- The podcast is daily for a couple of reasons: to help you feel better and more powerful every day, not occasionally, since every day becomes who you are and the life you experience; and personally, to dramatically impact your life and as many other lives as possible, what is done every single day has to shift
- Whatever you want to change, start with every single day, and don't make it negotiable. To get in shape, work out every single day: not the same thing, not crushing yourself nonstop, some days active recovery or whatever. Not three times a week, not a plan, but: "this hasn't been a part of every single day who I am. But going forward, it's gonna be a part of every single day who I am"
- Made every day, it becomes who you are instead of something that you do, from working out, eating and your relationship to being a father, your kids, your business, your health. Personally, nothing in life is more important than being a father. Communicating with your spouse, or caring for your body, goes from a few times a week to part of who you are
- That's when the really powerful changes happen: you look back and see how dramatically different that part of life used to be. From experience, personally, what leads there is every day
- Not "hey honey, we should sit down once a week and talk", not three workouts this week, not attention to your kids two days this week because stress lets you focus only on those two. Not three, two or five times a week. Make it every day, non-negotiable, and you will see tremendous change and impact
