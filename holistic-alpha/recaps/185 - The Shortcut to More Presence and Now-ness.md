> **TL;DR** — Presence brings happiness, effectiveness and better relationships, but there is no shortcut: it is a lifelong practice of learning, cold exposure, meditation, eyes-closed training, fasting and attention, always coming back; it also improves sex, and your body and surroundings bring you back.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 185, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Three reasons to care about presence, being in the moment, "now-ness". We all want these, and being fully present improves all of them:
  - Happiness: more present, you're happier, more at peace, more content, period. The more we get out of the present moment, into the past or the future, the more unhappy we are, typically
  - Effectiveness: in a workout, at your job, in a hobby, more in the moment means more effective. With your mind on tomorrow, on a month ago or on yesterday's conversation with your wife, a creative task at work suffers
  - Relationships: every single interaction and relationship, with your kids, friends, spouse, girlfriend or the cashier at the grocery store, benefits from you being more fully present
- The title kind of misleads: **there is no shortcut** in life. So often we look for one, to losing the weight, getting healthier, building the business. We all need a reminder at times: shortcuts are so tempting, and they do not exist, they are a lie
- **Quote:** Jocko Willink, probably originally from his podcast (his work is amazing, and highly suggested): "The shortcut is a lie. The hack doesn't get you there." The easy road "will not take you where you want to be. Stronger, smarter, faster, healthier, better, free." Reaching goals, overcoming obstacles and being the best version of you possible will not happen by itself, by cutting corners or by looking for the easy way: "There is no easy way. There is only hard work, late nights, early mornings, practice, rehearsal, repetition, study, sweat, blood, toil, frustration, and discipline."
- That applies to every aspect of our lives, and certainly to presence
- There are tools, absolutely, but like anything else, presence is a lifelong practice: do the things that lead to it every day and focus on being present every day. No switch makes you present every moment for the rest of your life
- Learn what presence really is: we often don't fully understand it, and even if we do, not really how to get back there, or some of the strategies within our own mind and body to get there. A number of books do a really good job; the personal favorite, recommended often and highly, truly life-changing for a lot of people who read it or heard the audiobook: *The Power of Now* by Eckhart Tolle, who has a number of really good books
- Cold exposure, a cold shower, an ice bath, any kind, is really powerful for fully bringing you into the moment: in a cold shower you're not thinking about next week's bill, tomorrow's project or a conversation from two days ago. It brings you 100% to that moment
- One of the most powerful ways to more presence and awareness is habits that bring us to that place: the more often we come back to it, the more often we can return to the now through the day. Cold exposure on a regular basis helps carry presence into moment-to-moment life
- Daily meditation: of its so many benefits, one is presence that carries through the rest of your day, especially once it's a daily habit. It doesn't have to take forever. **Story:** a 45-minute meditation that morning, awesome; a lot of days, about 10 minutes. 10 minutes a day will honestly change your life in so many ways, one being more presence
- **Eyes-closed physical training**: close your eyes and move, for the majority or the entire time, with really almost any exercise: certainly bodyweight (push-ups, pull-ups, squats), yoga, kettlebells, resistance bands. When we close our eyes to the outer world, we open them to what's happening inside, in mind, spirit and body, which brings us back into the present moment in a really powerful way. It has lots of other benefits, covered before and more to come
- Fasting, especially a longer fast: two or three days or longer is really incredible for bringing you to a connected, tapped-in, present place, tapped into the universe and the energy flowing through you. Day three of a water fast is typically when people really experience mental clarity, groundedness and presence. Daily practices bring us back to presence; occasional ones bring us there really powerfully, and having accessed that place makes it easier to come back to
- This seems to apply to intermittent fasting too: eating has a big impact on mind and emotions, and cutting back your daily eating window has so many benefits, probably including more presence. Not as powerful as a three-day fast, but it certainly helps
- Of the so many things you can do, another is simply intention: especially once you've learned and experienced full presence, just paying attention to whether you're really present is a huge part of it, maybe the biggest. Realizing you're lost in the future or the past takes presence: in that moment, you are present. So pay attention day to day and come back
- No matter how adept you get, you're always going to get pulled out of presence:
  - Until we learn about this and practice it, the majority of us are truly fully present very, very little, maybe 1% of the time, literally probably 99% of the time or more not really fully present, caught up in our head
  - We all get brief glimpses: some sport, a hobby that brings a flow state, sex. **Story:** rock climbing has always done that
  - With practice (reading *The Power of Now*, knowing how presence feels and how to come back, meditating every day), you gradually up that percentage, to 10%, 20%, 30%, maybe eventually 60, 70, 80, 90% of the time, something like that, but never 100%: it's just not the way it works
  - Like being on the path of discipline, improving and executing everything every single day: we're never on it 100% of the time, we always fall off, and what matters is getting back on. It's always up to you to notice you got swept up in thoughts and come back
- Presence also means better sex: one of the very, very best things you can do for it is to learn, practice and be fully present. You've probably had sex caught up in worry about performing or lasting, in work stress or in a conversation with your partner two days ago, all of which gets in the way big time of an enjoyable, connected, powerful experience
- There are other ways to be present, but these are some of the best and most powerful, suggested for use: meditate, use cold exposure, learn about presence and read *The Power of Now*, use eyes-closed exercise. You may find others that work for you
- Get into your body and your physical surroundings, touched on in *The Power of Now* and an occasional reminder here. Not present means lost in your head, in thoughts of the past and the future; body and surroundings are two really good ways back
  - Body: close your eyes and move, or don't even close them, just pay attention to your body. In the car as you drive, right now: feel the weight of your body on the seat, your feet on the ground, your arms on the door or the console, your hands on the steering wheel. Attention to your physical senses brings your awareness out of your head, the past and the future
  - Surroundings: really look around. So often we move about only sort of semi-aware of what's actually around us, because a lot of our awareness is lost in thoughts. Touch the things around you, look at them, really pay attention to what's physically in front of and around you right now
- Anything worth getting has no shortcut, just the discipline of taking the steps, one foot in front of the other, and presence is the same. Put some of these into practice, remember how beneficial presence is, and don't underestimate the power of fully being in the now
- **Story:** for years and years, hearing people or reading every now and then about being present always just lived as "that sounds good". When you really dive into this and bring it into your life in a powerful way, it is incredible, the difference it makes in your world: your inner world, how you feel, your body and health, your relationships, your effectiveness
