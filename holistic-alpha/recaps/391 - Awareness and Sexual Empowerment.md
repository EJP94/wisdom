  - In physical training we must push into serious discomfort: that is where physical growth happens, and also mental, spiritual and emotional growth
  - Also the uncomfortable conversations, and the uncomfortable choice in your job and career path to push out into an unknown

A request: evaluate your days and routines and step more into discomfort, where all growth happens and also really where we find peace: we are most at peace when striving and building something meaningful

A lot of people with incredible success often describe some of their most challenging times as some of their happiest, because they were striving. Pushing our edges leads to a deeper peace and contentment

- **Awareness** is one of the key factors in all of this, clear from the personal journey and from working with guys. It has a couple of aspects: what we give our attention to and focus on, and our understanding. It plays out in every stage of sexual empowerment:

  - Semen retention: awareness of what goes on in your body, of how your sexual energy builds and moves and how you can control it
  - More advanced levels: using sexual energy to create things in your life takes a much richer, advanced kind of awareness of that energy, how it moves and affects you and how to guide and control it
- Awareness is really the root of all growth, and certainly of sexual empowerment. So meditation is recommended "over and over and over and over again": not the only way to build awareness, but a crucial one. Awareness, attention or presence: our ability to guide and understand what our mind is focused on. Seen with so many guys: consistent meditation becomes a big driver of change, as they grow more aware of their mind, body and attention
- Your ability to guide your attention and to understand what happens inside you, mentally, emotionally and physically, is a huge, huge factor. Have a meditation practice and be consistent: ten minutes a day, over a long period of time, can have a huge life-changing impact on multiple aspects of your life, "including and especially sexual empowerment"
- Whether you're a young guy trying to give up porn or further along, translating sexual energy into creating things, awareness is a huge key: practice guiding your attention and being aware of your mind, emotions and body. **Story:** still on this journey too
- **Mindful moments**, as he calls them, reconnect you with being aware and present, and a really great way is through your physical senses. A few times throughout the day, for 30 seconds or a minute, pause and:
  - Feel the weight of your feet on the ground, or your legs on the chair, and the air on your skin
  - Pay attention to what you're smelling, actually to what you're seeing, really to what you're hearing
  - Touch something while actually looking at it and being aware of it
  - Feel your breath
  - So often we're lost in our head, but we experience life through our physical senses, not our head, so reconnecting with them is one of the most basic ways to build awareness
- How impactful a meditation practice will be over time can't be emphasized strongly enough. You can meditate longer, in lots of ways; whatever it looks like, have practices that build awareness (or attention, focus, presence: words with sort of nuanced meanings)

Sex is the biggest thing that affects all three, so in whichever area you focus or struggle, sexual empowerment is at the root of the most powerful changes: harnessing your sexuality is the most powerful thing you have access to, whatever the challenge
