> **TL;DR** — Happiness, your own and what you bring others, is the only thing that matters and the measure of any alpha male; it takes brave, harder short-term choices against resistance, so spend 15 minutes every day on what will bring the most joy.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 21, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- What matters more than anything is happiness, yours and what you bring others: "that's the only thing that matters", that and making memories. Stuff, being in shape, a good sex life, awesome kids, the trappings of the modern world are nice, but nothing else matters but your happiness
- Miserable in a job that pays for your lifestyle, or unhappy in a relationship you're not brave enough to end: is that pursuing happiness in a way that feels true on the deepest level? Maybe not. Knocking out a job, relationship or habit that isn't contributing to our happiness sometimes takes bravery
- Happiness is not easy: long-term happiness is not doing whatever feels good in the moment, and a lot of times it's the opposite. Sleeping in and sitting around all day might feel good in the moment, but leaves you feeling worse and worse the longer you do it; not facing what you need to face in a relationship leaves you more and more unhappy; job hunting or building a business to escape an unhappy job might not feel good, but it's what long-term happiness takes
- **Happiness is the measure**, the ultimate measure of any alpha male, not the stuff, the people you know or the money. Only you know, within your own life and mind, how to be the most happy and bring the most happiness to others
- That's part of why we're here, and seemingly a huge role of our job as men: bringing happiness to those we love, our family, which sometimes means the hard short-term thing. If they're missing the hell out of you because you're gone all the time at a job you hate, do something about it. Miserable because you're out of shape? Prioritize your long-term happiness
- The more true joy lies behind something, most often the more resistance you'll face getting to it. Either we accept facing resistance over and over and move towards that thing anyway, or we let it stand in the way and die with regret. Be brave
- **Story:** years, in so many different areas, were spent unhappy from not being willing to face resistance and pursue happiness
- Happiness doesn't just happen. We choose to be happy kind of moment to moment, it seems: no matter how bad things are, we can always kind of choose our attitude. Long-term happiness is up to us too: choosing a path that creates happiness for us and others, the only thing we'll take with us
- You could die where you stand, in the fractions of a second after getting hit by a bus. Pursuing our true happiness, as the guidepost and measure of our path, is the way to a life without regret. Deep joy comes a lot of times from harder short-term choices that aren't necessarily the easy choice
- We all, it seems, have some aspect of our life we know inherently we truly want to change, for a different level of joy and satisfaction for us and maybe those around us, but we let resistance stand in the way. Choose happiness, and go after whatever is eating at you
- The 15 minutes talked about a couple of days ago: every day, 15 minutes toward what will bring the greatest increase in joy for you and those around you, like submitting job applications, planning and building your own business, or setting a timer to talk and figure stuff out in a relationship
- Really fit but miserable? Who cares. In fitness, a job or a relationship, "quote unquote success" without true happiness on a deep core level is failure, not success
- You have more resistance-facing power than you've ever felt before. Today, take some positive step in an area of your life where you haven't been: figure out what holds you back from the most happiness and get after changing it
