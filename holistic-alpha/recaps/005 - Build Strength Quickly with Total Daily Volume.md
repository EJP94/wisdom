> **TL;DR** — To build strength quickly, target total daily volume: pick a set of push-ups, squats or pull-ups you can do every time, repeat it 20 to 50 times through the day, for example every 15 minutes, and start today.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 5, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- We all want to be stronger: it feels good and, especially as men, it brings a different energy to life
- Personally, one of the very favorite ways, amazing in how well it works for building strength quickly, is focusing on **total daily volume** of activity, especially of a couple of simple activities
- With push-ups, personally one of the very favorite things to do:
  - Pick a set that pushes you a little but is pretty reasonable, one you can basically do every time, not your absolute max: say 10, if you'd max out at 15, 20 or 25
  - Do 20, 30, 40 or 50 sets of 10 through the day, starting in the morning, every 15 or 30 minutes, however you want, a set of 10 or two
  - The longer the period, the better, from waking to bedtime if you can. It doesn't necessarily have to be every 15 minutes
- Each set activates those muscles and seems to give them a little signal to grow. A full workout of half an hour to two hours needs at least until the next day to recover. Mini sets aren't intense and don't really break you down: they just say "keep growing", and your body kind of heals a little and grows a little. At least that's the way it seems to work, experientially
- If you've never tried it, try it for a week, or three days, even one or two. If you can do five push-ups in a set, aim for at least a hundred for the day, 20 sets of five as a minimum: over five hours, that's a set every 15 minutes, with a timer
- Total volume builds really quickly this way, and you'll probably surprise yourself with how much you can do and how fast strength grows
- Other exercises: bodyweight or Hindu squats, really excellent for the lower body (if you've never heard of Hindu squats, they work really well for this), or pull-ups, if you can do a set of at least one or two or three. You can mix it up
- Pick something you can just get down and do. Squats or push-ups are personally favored because they work anywhere: 10 push-ups take maybe 20 seconds, in the lunchroom at work or at home. After work, 5 to 10 p.m. works too, if you're really diligent with a timer every 15 minutes
- If you've never tried it, you'll be amazed how quickly you get stronger. It also seems an awesome way to set a new sort of record for yourself, and it adjusts your perspective
- **Story:** never having really done this, or that many push-ups, the goal was a thousand push-ups a day for probably about a week, not reached every day. Surely some guys can do way more, and probably a lot of people can't do one, so the number doesn't matter. What mattered was that a thousand in one workout seemed completely unreachable, yet broken into little chunks through the day, it adds up powerfully
- Every day has 96 15-minute increments. Use even half or a quarter of them for one set of something each, and it's amazing how quickly you get stronger
- Push-ups are such a basic, functional strength thing that everybody seems able to benefit from more of them, whatever your sport or however you want to improve your body or fitness
- The bottom line: lots of sets through the day, targeting a big total daily volume number, 20 to 50 sets of whatever number you can do, varying the hours and the minutes in between. Make each set hard enough to kind of push you while you can still do it every time. By the 40th or 50th set, if you do that many, they obviously get harder. You'll probably be amazed at the results
- If it's early enough in the day, try it today: there's such power in today, so don't let it be a tomorrow thing. Even late, kick it off today, then try it tomorrow. It costs one day of effort, and in reality you'll lose nothing and gain a ton. Push-ups, pull-ups and squats all work great, and the recommendation is push-ups, with a high number for the day
