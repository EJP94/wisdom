> **TL;DR** — NoFap lumps porn, masturbation and ejaculation together, preaches removal and offers no plan; conscious retention sets your ejaculation frequency by your charge, longer when depleted, more often when very charged, with regular conscious activity and listening to your body.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 731, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- You can figure out your ideal ejaculation frequency at any moment in life. First, a couple of reminders of why **NoFap** sucks
- A lot of guys equate semen retention and NoFap, but they differ in very key ways. NoFap's basic intention, helping guys get more from their sexuality and heal its toxicity, is good; its methodology seems in a lot of cases to do more harm than good
- **It throws the baby out with the bathwater.** At its core NoFap is about quitting porn, which can be very toxic: a great idea. But lumping porn, masturbation and ejaculation together as one thing is fundamentally flawed: we cannot possibly heal and optimize our sexuality that way. They are three separate things: does each have a place in my life, and what does that look like?
- A relatively small study, just seen (there haven't been a lot): 28 or 29% of guys who attempted NoFap hard mode had increased suicidal thoughts in whatever the period was. Take it with a grain of salt. But many, many guys have voiced great and deep frustration and pain applying that flawed approach
- **It preaches removal**, complete elimination for the most part, instead of healthy interaction. Going 60 days, 90 days or forever without ejaculating, or never masturbating again: that all-or-nothing approach is not only ineffective but harmful, like hitting our head against a brick wall: "we're gonna lose that every time"
- Plenty of guys see, in a lot of cases, temporary improvements: ejaculating multiple times a day, then starting any retention, even hard mode, removes a huge drain. But flawed fundamental principles are by definition not sustainable: eventually they rear their ugly head, with a hugely negative impact. That's what happens, heard over and over from guys who bought into hard mode and this PMO thing. It's painful and does not work long term: "it truly doesn't"
- **It provides no actual plan.** Never doing such a fundamental part of us again is not a plan to harness this incredible power. In the NoFap forums, how to tap into our sexuality with activity, stimulation and pleasure, in a powerful, expansive way, is not talked about at all
- **Conscious semen retention** is much, much better. At a basic level it simply means making an intentional choice about ejaculation: how often, under what circumstances, and masturbation versus sex does kind of play in. Retention is, without question, a game changer, but only, especially long term, with an approach that works: conscious retention, understanding the impact and adjusting
- Removing porn is a fantastic idea, almost a prerequisite: you can't really practice conscious semen retention while porn has any place in your life. But it's not the end-all be-all: hundreds of guys spoken with who eliminated porn, largely or completely, still have frustrations, challenges or room to grow in their sexuality and retention. Neither getting rid of porn nor swearing off PMO is a magic bullet
- Conscious retention means understanding the principles, then figuring out what is right for you now
- **Taoist formulas.** The most ancient texts going deepest into retention and this energy are the Taoist teachings, with a few formulas depending on the text:
  - By the number of times having sex: for every ten times, maybe ejaculate two or three times
  - By the seasons, in some texts: a certain amount in summer, fall and spring, and in winter maybe not at all
  - By age, in some other texts: your teens, 20s, 30s, 40s, 50s, 60s and so on determine how often to ejaculate
- Much more useful than any formula, it seems, are guidelines for finding your own, since it is unique to the individual and depends completely on where you are in life and in the journey
- **Very charged.** If your libido, sexual energy, fire, charge, qi or jing, depending on how we talk about these things, is very charged up, whether from youth, habits or your unique body, it absolutely makes sense to ejaculate more often. Longer is not always better: at a certain point the benefits cease and it can start to be a drag. Not ejaculating enough, constantly bumping into that wall, you get some kind of stagnation, an almost stuck state, by artificially blocking the flow
- **Very depleted.** If your charge is very low, from a number of factors or most simply from excessive ejaculation for years or decades, it makes sense to go much, much longer. Weigh your charge now and over time: long-term depletion, or a very powerful charge built over years?
- For a vast majority of guys, at the beginning and maybe for a good period, longer is, in general terms, kind of better: it is very common to have energetic catching up to do after likely years of excessive ejaculation. So, in essence, go as long as you can: you won't go forever anyway, between accidental releases, wet dreams and sex. It may be very beneficial to go not only a couple of weeks but maybe a month, two or three. As your baseline energy builds, that time frame might change
- **The drop-off.** Early on, even after 60 or 90 days, a big drop-off after release is your indication to keep going at least that long or longer: you're coming back to a depleted state. Feeling essentially no change, or even better, a little more frequent might be better. That's definitely a smaller percentage of guys, but it very well might be you: if after years of retention you no longer really feel the drop-off, and maybe feel kind of stuck or frustrated, it may be time to shorten those time frames
- A request: think about your long-term energetic position. Barely started, never gone more than a day or two, or coming from long-term depletion? Go as long as you can. With the podcast about five years old, if you're a number of years in, keep evaluating the right timing, as it likely will change. That is conscious retention: deciding, evaluating, understanding, then choosing
- Retention is always going to be helped by conscious stimulation and activity, whether a solo mindful edging practice or sex with your partner while retaining. In personal experience, in virtually every case retention is more beneficial with regular activity. Hard mode, avoiding activity, is not the way: we don't use it, we lose it
- Heard from so many guys: disciplined on NoFap, they went three, six, nine, twelve months, some for years, without releasing or any activity, single, no porn, expecting hard mode to be a magic unlock. Then they get a girlfriend and find their erections and control are terrible, their libido sometimes almost non-existent. If you don't use it, you will lose it: harness it through conscious activity and conscious retention
- The right ejaculation frequency is not the same for me or for you, nor for you today, two years ago or two years from now. Keep evaluating how you feel when you release, go longer or go shorter: the sustainable approach is finding what works best for your body now
- **Ejaculation is not bad.** It is a beautiful, powerful, incredible thing, but like many powerful things it needs care: that is really the difference. Seen and heard in so many guys today: a willy-nilly carelessness or worse. It's not ignorance: on a deeper level a lot of guys recognize its power and approach it carelessly anyway, a big, big problem
- Don't get caught up in "this is right for them and therefore it's right for me". Understand the basic principles, listen to your body and energy, and watch those gauges to find the right frequency
