> **TL;DR** — Kegels, pelvic floor exercises that boost sex life and life force, work best done every day, focused on the rear IC muscle near the coccyx, and mixed with at least as many reverse Kegels, the biggest help for lasting longer.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 18, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- **Kegels** are exercises of the pelvic floor muscles, and they seem to get nowhere near enough talk among men and in men's health circles. Strengthening that whole area makes a tremendous difference in your sex life, absolutely, and in so many ways it really boosts sort of your core energy and life force
- Three keys:
  - **Consistency**: you only see a benefit if you do them on a regular basis. A couple of times a week, "oh, I should do some Kegels for a minute", and you might as well not even worry about it. Like anything else in life, the real benefit comes when you do it every day: make it a daily routine. **Story:** tally marks, in an app called Tally App on the phone, track the most important daily habits, and they're great for the do-it-one-to-five-times-a-day kind of things
  - **Focus on the rear**: different ways of doing Kegels have dramatically different impacts, especially on your sex life. Three muscles are involved: the bulbocavernosus (BC), the pubococcygeus (PC) and the iliococcygeus (IC). The technical names don't really matter: what matters is where they sit and which you focus on
    - Going from the front to the back, and up as you go back: the BC is the most in front, kind of under your balls; the PC is in the middle; the IC sits further back, against the coccyx bone, so when you squeeze it you can almost feel it in your super low back
    - Focusing forward, on the PC and especially the BC, is much less effective for controlling your arousal level, with a much different impact on your sex life than the deeper, rear muscles. Personally found: the deeper and more towards the back, the bigger the muscles, with greater control; the smaller BC and PC a lot of times don't give that ability, and without the biggest, most powerful muscles, the IC towards the rear, you don't get the same impact
    - So, highly suggested: still imagine stopping the flow of urine, or holding in a fart, but feel the squeeze towards the back, against the coccyx bone in your super low back, the ideal place. That gives much greater control over your arousal, and sort of a bigger life energy impact than the BC, just under your nuts
  - **Both Kegels and reverse Kegels**: reverse Kegels are incredibly impactful for the overall health of the pelvic floor, and really, really impactful for your sex life. Kegels strengthen the pelvic floor, for better control, stronger orgasms and more life force energy; mixing in reverse Kegels gives much more control over your arousal level. If one part of Kegels helps you last longer in bed more than anything else, it's reverse Kegels. Do at least as many reverse Kegels as Kegels
    - A reverse Kegel squeezes the same muscles the other way, ideally again towards the rear, though you really want to exercise all of them: imagine pushing out a fart or peeing harder. Mixing both is a whole different world from Kegels alone, a huge difference
- If you haven't been doing Kegels, you will be truly amazed at what just a week or two of consistent practice can do for the way you feel, and definitely for your sex life
- Kegels seem super important for all men, a topic to keep talking about, and other men hopefully will too, as they really improve a number of aspects of your life. However good your sex life is now, probably all of us could use even more and better there. But it's not just sexual performance: your sex energy and the strength of these muscles feed into your life force energy, your physical energy for other physical stuff and your drive, and many aspects of us are impacted
- A recommended app: Stamena, used on an iPhone, with probably an Android version too. Essentially a timer meant for Kegel practice, it beeps and takes you through both Kegels and reverse Kegels in progressively more challenging routines. App or not, definitely do Kegels, lots of them, and you will feel the power
