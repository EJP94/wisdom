> **TL;DR** — Before battling resistance, a meditative moment, sort of a pregame ritual, one deep breath with eyes closed, shifts mind and body into the energy of what comes next, made more powerful, it seems, by a daily 10- or 15-minute meditation practice.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 25, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- We all battle resistance within ourselves, the enemy that keeps us from making the changes we want and progressing the way we want, unless we battle it, face it and overcome it: a day-by-day battle we always face. Personally, huge power found in the **meditative moment** before kind of doing battle with it
- Warriors throughout the ages, entrepreneurs, elite-level athletes and so many others excelling in life have used sort of a pregame ritual to their advantage. The ability and intention to shift your mind state into a new place, to attack things in a new way, is hugely powerful
- **Story:** one of the major changes found in life was meditation, always at least once a day before the day's workout: coffee, then 10 or 15 minutes of meditation, and as it ends the coffee is just kicking in, so the workout follows. That routine alone made a huge difference in life, really in psychology, and certainly in training
- **Story:** climbing pretty much every day, the favorite moment is finishing tying the climbing shoes: a pause, holding both feet for a minute, eyes closed, a deep breath, settling in. That act of stopping for a deep breath shifts gears into warrior mode: shoes on, headphones in, attack. First the meditation before, now also that moment, made a great difference in training, and it gets applied throughout the day
- It can benefit you so much as you shift gears from one thing to the next: from work to being with your kids or your spouse when you get home, or from hanging out with your buddies to being with your girlfriend. Taking just a minute before the new thing to close your eyes and take a deep breath really helps shift mind and body into the energy of what's next
- Sometimes that means settling down. **Story:** often the same coming out of a workout, so amped up, physically at a high level of movement and speed, that a moment like that is sometimes needed to shift back into a lower sort of energy wavelength, to deal with people out of that intense workout state
- A tremendously powerful tool, seemingly made more powerful if you meditate, which gives such power and health to mind and body. With a daily 10 or 15-minute practice, one deep breath, eyes closed, before shifting gears calls back and reenacts the skill: sort of resetting and coming back to that blue sky place, whatever clouds are floating around in your head. The more we practice it intentionally for 10 or 15 minutes, the more we can call on it in a breath or two
- Use it before a workout, a phone call you're super nervous for, maybe facing your boss in some kind of gnarly meeting, a relationship conversation, or in your day-to-day routine: whatever you kind of want to be best at or need help dealing with. Between aspects of life, that meditative moment is really powerful for helping us kind of recenter and be in a good place for the next activity. Give it a shot
- **Story:** most of life was spent not meditating, and life was fine, but it's so much better with meditation. "It just is": like the best 10 or 15 minutes a day you could possibly spend. So many years went into letting it be a "maybe I'll do that one day": don't do the same, just start now. Whether 25 or 65, give meditation a week or two and you'll be feeling the benefits already
