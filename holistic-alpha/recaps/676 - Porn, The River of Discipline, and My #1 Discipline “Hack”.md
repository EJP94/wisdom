> **TL;DR** — Porn, probably the most anti-disciplined thing we can do, has you swimming upstream in the river of discipline; a cold shower, the number one discipline hack, puts you downstream, and anything that sucks but serves the long term helps rebuild discipline when leaving porn.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 676, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Usually not really a fan of the word "hack": it seems not to really speak to a larger intention, and is probably overused. But it seems a good one here, for this massive return on a comparatively small thing
- Porn is probably the most anti-disciplined thing we can do, literally its polar opposite: it trains us in the exact opposite
- **Discipline** is prioritizing long-term goals, visions, commitments and desires, sacrificing moment to moment to serve them. Porn says "give me what I want right now", forget the future: maybe not consciously, but basically
- A lot of you have seen other areas slip, maybe for a long time, deep in the porn habit. Conversely, personally seen over and over again: men who get out of porn and heal see so many areas come into line, discipline being one reason
- **The river of discipline.** With porn, discipline elsewhere is literally swimming upstream: possible at times, sure, but way, way harder. Without porn, sort of a discipline momentum carries you downstream, "and it will"
- Discipline is often made out, it seems, as hard, and a lot of men see it so. It's really not: it's momentum, like every other area of life, which makes continuing much, much easier
- The number one discipline hack: the **cold shower**. **Story:** done every day, without missing one in probably four or five years, except maybe a day or two camping in hot weather without access. A game changer
- Beyond physical benefits (immune system, other physiological ones) and an incredible emotional reset, personally the number one reason for sticking with them seems to be that they keep one on track the most with discipline, because they always suck. The after is enjoyable, as is knowing how good it will be for the day; the first moment stepping in isn't ever really
- **Story:** starting the day with one, at four, five or maybe nine after sleeping in, meant "boom", swimming downstream in the river of discipline, making other disciplined choices so much easier
- A challenge: though other habits can have a big impact individually, find one more disastrous for your discipline than porn, or a two-minute one as positive for it as cold showers
- In coaching, many men, back to the first Bootcamp program years ago, have used cold showers in a foundation of habits to rebuild their discipline and stay away from porn. It works both ways: no porn eases disciplined choices, and disciplined choices ease staying off porn
- Do not underestimate choices of maybe a couple of minutes, or 10, 15 or 20 minutes of porn: they can be disastrous for your discipline. Ejaculating has its own major energetic impact, of course, but merely watching is basically going the opposite direction of discipline
- Early in moving away from porn, or soon after, one of the most important things to do is rebuild your discipline, or build it even stronger. Not that you have none: none of us is perfect, but we absolutely have ranges we can move in
- Anything that sucks but that you know is good for you long term, a cold shower or otherwise, has a massive impact on discipline. Something pointless that sucks, like hitting your head against a tree, won't help: it serves nothing long term
