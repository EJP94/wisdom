> **TL;DR** — Sexual stamina is neither mystical nor just Kegels: everything comes down to three dials (a relaxed pelvic floor, a slow, nasal, restful breath, and awareness that stays present and spreads through the body), all three needed, none needing to be perfect.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 813, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- Many men think stamina is extremely complex, maybe even mystical or unattainable. Some Kegel apps advertised on social media suggest the opposite, that Kegels alone give powerful control. The truth is in between: all the techniques and nuances boil down to **three dials**
- **Why stamina matters:**
  - More pleasure: better control means more pleasure
  - Managing sexual energy and semen retention: to harness that force, you need to choose when or if you ejaculate
  - Deeper, more meaningful connection with your partner
- **Dial 1: the pelvic floor**, or root muscle, a group of muscles treated as one
  - In the moment, it mostly comes down to keeping the pelvic floor relaxed, even at high arousal
  - Tension in the root muscle, and especially involuntary contractions, are a primary cause of premature ejaculation
  - With weak erections, a lot of men run into a tendency to squeeze the pelvic floor to hold or strengthen the erection. So relaxing at high arousal requires strong erections, which many factors can affect, from sleep to food, weight and stress
  - Strong erections also need a strong pelvic floor, so Kegels matter. A strong root muscle gives stronger erections, a stronger libido and more sexual charge
  - Practice: Kegels and reverse Kegels (more in the show's archive), with a relaxed pelvic floor as the base state you return to. Notice involuntary contractions and release them, over and over
- **Dial 2: the breath**, less known among men
  - Breath shapes the balance of the nervous system between the parasympathetic (restful) and sympathetic (fight or flight) states. It is never fully one or the other, always a balance, and breath is one of the strongest factors in where it lands
  - The sympathetic side brings muscle tension and more automatic body responses, so less conscious control, and it reduces focus and presence. A relaxed pelvic floor is very hard in that state
  - The parasympathetic side brings presence, conscious control and relaxed muscles
  - As few as two or three short, shallow, rapid chest breaths can push you toward fight or flight, and a couple of minutes of it can really push you further. If that is your default as arousal rises, or as sex starts, you get tension, a harder time relaxing the pelvic floor, less presence and less control
  - Deep, slow, nasal breathing moves you toward the restful state
  - Practice:
    - Notice your breathing moment to moment. Few people pay attention to or train it, though breath is literally what gives us life. Breathing on its own doesn't mean breathing well
    - Stay in nasal breathing, lips sealed, at high arousal and during exercise. That alone slows and deepens the breath
    - Breath work of any kind, from box breathing to Wim Hof breathing, with many books on it
    - In edging sessions, notice at 7, 8 or 9 on the arousal scale whether your breath gets fast and shallow, and consciously slow it down
- **Dial 3: awareness**, which even fewer men think about or practice. It is "more powerful than you can possibly imagine"
  - Presence: awareness caught in anxious thoughts, stress, another place and time, or porn disconnects you from your body, so you can't manage your breath or your pelvic floor
  - The moment: are you truly aware of your partner, of what you feel, of the energy moving between you, and of what happens in your body?
  - Inside the body, "where your awareness goes, energy flows". Focusing on a part of the body tends to bring sensation there and, in essence, channels energy toward it
  - By habit, many men put all their awareness on the point of stimulation, the sexual center. If awareness is overly focused there, all the energy channels there, and it can hold only so much before it overflows
  - Bring awareness to the sacrum, spine, crown of the head or soles of the feet. You feel sensation there and channel energy through the whole body, which can hold vastly more sexual energy than the sexual center alone
  - Practice: be present through the day, letting go of past and future and getting out of your head. Meditate: its benefits "cannot possibly" be overstated, and it teaches you where your awareness is and how to place it. In edging and sex, check: am I present or off in a fantasy, and where in my body is my awareness? How you practice matters less than practicing
- **All three dials are needed.** One is not enough. You can do all the Kegels you want, build a strong pelvic floor and even relax it at high arousal, and still lack control without breath and awareness. If a ton of Kegels seem to make no difference at all, part of the reason is most likely missing breath and awareness. Even with two out of three, losing the breath into short, shallow breathing tenses everything and you release when you don't want to
- Sort everything you have practiced or heard into the three dials, since everything about sexual control falls into one of them. During sex, these are the three things to tune into moment by moment
- **Good is good enough.** No dial needs mastery. A favorite phrase: in life, good is good enough 90%, maybe 99%, of the time, and perfectionism is a common trap. Good-enough control of each looks like:
  - Pelvic floor: strong enough for strong erections, relaxed at high arousal
  - Breath: avoid short, shallow, rapid breathing, without needing "magical" slow breathing
  - Awareness: not totally lost, with some sense of where it is in the body and a good ability to stay present with yourself and your partner
- With good-enough control of all three, you can have the control you want, choosing if or when you ejaculate no matter how high arousal gets. A lot of men say they get discouraged after years of trying things that don't work. Understanding these three dials changes that, "I promise you": the control can be limitless, no matter what's happening with your partner
- Keep stacking bricks: the biggest, most beautiful castles are built one small brick at a time, and so are life and sexual stamina
