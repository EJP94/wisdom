*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 175, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- This is Workout Cast number one, a sexual health multi-pack, and the first episode of the show broadcast in video as well
- Mondays are an awesome day: a fresh start, a chance to do something new, maybe get back on track. In that spirit, the show does something all new
- **Story:** workout videos posted on Telegram, LinkedIn and Facebook are usually one of two things: a section of clips showing different kinds of movements, or longer workouts sped up, say, eight, ten or twelve times, to condense them so the movement can kind of be seen in a shorter period of time. For this episode, the day's training session was filmed from start to finish instead: pretty much nonstop, about an hour and 20 minutes, a really good session. Over it, the video plays five previous episodes of the podcast back to back, all kind of centered around sexual health, five that seem to go well together
- It is good to see things in real time. The session used a variety of speeds, some movements really slow, some faster, and watching in real time gives a better idea of that
- **Story:** the session also held a lot of different movement, different things than usual: a lot of primal movement, some capoeira, some resistance band work, some tree work on Wilma [?], a lot of tiger push-ups instead of regular push-ups, handstands and cartwheels
- Listening to the audio is, of course, fine, and probably still brings benefit from hearing five episodes back to back that kind of go well together. The hope for the video version: keep an eye on the training session while listening, and hopefully get some ideas and some inspiration from it
- The five, in the order played: *Sexual Energy and the Battle Against Resistance* (announced as episode 110), *Holistically Diagnosing Erection Problems* (announced as episode 123), *Semen Retention vs. NoFap* (announced as episode 92), *3 Daily Practices to Be a Multi-Orgasmic Man* (announced as episode 38, way back) and *Kegels for Men Revisited* (announced as episode 90)
