> **TL;DR** — Resistance, the inner force that holds every man back, never dies and is beaten by growing stronger: in body, hormones, fasting, sex energy and mind, so a man dominates resistance rather than other people.

*Every idea and story below is Steven Mathis's, from Holistic Alpha episode 1. Not fact-checked.*

- The universe runs on a **law of duality**: opposing forces that balance each other without cancelling out, like light and dark. It applies inside us too
- Inside every man there is a drive toward his dreams and who he is meant to be, and an opposing force: **resistance**
  - It is not other people or anything in the physical world. It lives inside you, "living and breathing" every day
  - It pushes back: it makes you stop instead of putting in the effort, or stay quiet in a relationship when you should speak up
  - It will be with you in every area of your life, and you cannot kill it
- Beating resistance is not about fixing each area one by one. Step back and strengthen who you are as a man from the inside out, so you meet resistance every day from a **position of strength**
  - Each win makes the next one easier: "the more he gets the message"
  - You probably beat resistance in some areas of life and lose to it in others
- The podcast has one purpose: to help men unleash their true power by strengthening the man they are, with daily strategies, tips, tools, knowledge and inspiration. Episodes are planned daily, mostly about 10–12 minutes each
- The fight seems to come down to a few areas:
  - **Physical body:** it affects your mind, emotions, relationships and business. If resistance beats you physically, it is like losing the first battle of the day and entering every other one weaker. Built through fitness, nutrition, the right supplements and hormone optimization
  - **Hormones:** "hormones affect everything and everything affects your hormones": how you feel, perceive, act and think, and your confidence. Optimizing hormones and testosterone puts you in the strongest position to beat resistance, and will be a recurring topic
  - **Fasting:** not a daily topic, but a "temporary superpower" against resistance
  - **Sex energy:** it matters beyond your sex life. It affects your life force, your drive to get things done, your confidence, physical energy and joy. At its root, it seems to affect how strong you are against resistance: the more you build it, the more it turns into strength against resistance. Whatever your relationship status (single, married, divorced), you can harness and build it over time or waste it, and wasting it drains you physically and of life force
  - **Mind:** without understanding and working with your mind, "none of that other stuff matters". The basics are attitude and mindset, training the mind to handle tough situations through the way you work out, and sitting still to meditate for a few minutes
- None of this is complicated, but it is not easy. Each area needs work every day
- Stop comparing your body, business or relationships to anyone else's. Aim to be better today than yesterday
- "We're not wolves." Being an **alpha male** is not about dominating other people but about dominating the resistance within you
