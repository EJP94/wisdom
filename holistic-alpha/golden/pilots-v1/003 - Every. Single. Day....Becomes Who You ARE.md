> **TL;DR** — What you do every single day becomes who you are, for better (compounding change) or worse (a daily fast-food diet that ended in "a disaster zone"), so any change should be daily and non-negotiable, which is also why the podcast is daily.

*Every idea and story below is Steven Mathis's, from Holistic Alpha episode 3. Not fact-checked.*

- "We are what we do on a daily basis." Who you are today is the product of what you have done **every single day**, kept up for weeks and months on end. Not of special events, things done three times a week, or something you do for two weeks and then stop
- Any attempt to change (get in shape, eat better, improve a relationship, add or subtract something) that is not built on a daily habit seems destined to fail. A daily habit compounds, and that is how big changes happen
- It works both ways. **Story:** early adulthood was a miserable time. The daily habits: a 7-Eleven chili hot dog for breakfast, fast food for lunch, McDonald's on the way home and, for a while almost every night, a Taco Bell caramel apple empanada topped with ice cream and caramel syrup. The result was "a disaster zone" physically and mentally
- **Story:** the podcast itself is daily for two reasons: to help listeners build daily habits, and because impacting as many lives as possible takes shifting one's own daily actions first
- **Make it non-negotiable.** If you need to get in shape, work out every day. That doesn't mean the same workout or crushing yourself every day: some days are active recovery. It is not a plan but a decision that this is now part of who you are
- A daily habit stops being something you do and becomes who you are: working out, eating better, talking with your spouse, being a father (personally, nothing matters more), and everything from your kids to your business to your health. That is when you look back and see how different life used to be
- The weekly versions don't work: "Honey, we should sit down once a week and talk", three workouts this week, or attention to your kids on only two days because stress leaves room for no more
