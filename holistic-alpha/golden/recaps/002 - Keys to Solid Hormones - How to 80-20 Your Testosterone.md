> **TL;DR** — The 80/20 of testosterone is three keys in each of four areas: sleep, stress and toxins; good fats, no toxic fats and no sugar; less ejaculation and rarely, if ever, alone, while staying sexually active; and daily activity, intensity and compound resistance training.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 2, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- The **80/20 principle**: 80% of results come from 20% of the effort, in everything "from our personal lives to business to milk production in Wisconsin". Recommended book: *80/20 Sales and Marketing* by Perry Marshall
- Three keys in each of four areas, in no particular order: which one pays off most depends on your circumstances
- **Lifestyle**
  - **Sleep.** Most hormone production happens during sleep. Too little sleep raises cortisol and stress hormones, which "don't coexist" with testosterone: the more stress hormones, generally the lower testosterone. Enough sleep gives a double benefit
  - **Stress.** High cortisol hurts testosterone. Sleep, meditation, exercise and a few supplements bring stress down. Favorite supplement for it: ashwagandha
  - **Toxins.** The most impactful one you are probably exposed to is fragrance: body wash, soap, shampoo, deodorant, cologne, anything with "fragrance" on the label. Fragrance is highly estrogenic and causes estrogen-like effects. Plastics and their chemicals are estrogenic toxins too. There are other toxins, but keeping fragrance and plastic out of your life as much as possible gives the most bang for the buck
- **Nutrition**
  - Other levers, in passing: magnesium, with pumpkin seeds as a source, and cruciferous vegetables like broccoli and cabbage, which remove excess estrogen. Nutrition seems to boil down to three main keys
  - **Enough good fats:** saturated and monounsaturated fats from clean sources, like coconut oil, olive oil, organic and sustainable palm oil, pasture-raised butter and ghee. Macadamias and pumpkin seeds are two of the only nuts and seeds that are personal favorites, for their fat profile and other reasons. The brain needs fat and the body makes hormones from it. Avoiding fat is a common mistake
  - **Avoid toxic fats "like the plague":** on a basic level, polyunsaturated fats, which are unstable and harmful to hormones. They are in fried food, fast food, packaged and processed food, and peanut, canola, vegetable and sunflower oil
  - **Cut sugar.** Sugar has a direct, significant negative effect on testosterone. In one study, men given a sugary syrup saw testosterone drop 25% and stay lower for hours. If you eat sugar daily, it is already hurting your hormones every time you eat a meaningful amount
- **Sex**
  - **Avoid excess ejaculation.** However it happens, frequent ejaculation drains your hormones and life energy, or "qi", as some people call it
  - **Stop ejaculating alone.** Solo ejaculation has the worst impact: avoid it entirely if possible, or do it very rarely
  - **Stay sexually active and harness the energy.** This is not about having no sex. Learn to ride the edge without ejaculating, and eventually to separate orgasm from ejaculation. The more semen you conserve, the more you feel it in your hormones, life force and mind. This applies with one partner, alone or with several
- **Fitness**
  - **Move every day.** Hormones are largely the body's signal-and-response system: meet a bear on a trail and adrenaline and cortisol spike. Just sitting signals that you don't need testosterone. Daily activity signals that you need to be strong, that you need testosterone and not too much estrogen
  - **Intensity.** Raising the intensity of workouts probably changes your hormonal response more than anything else. You can feel it: the harder you can go in a short time, the more hormonal benefit you feel. Fifteen minutes of hard sprints with short breaks beats an hour or an hour and a half of slow cardio. **Story:** alternating climbing and push-ups, hard and fast
  - **Resistance training.** Not heavy lifting for hours every day: a couple of sets with the right exercise can have amazing benefits. Favor compound, explosive, whole-body moves that load the lower body: squats, clean and jerk, kettlebell swings, kettlebell snatches
