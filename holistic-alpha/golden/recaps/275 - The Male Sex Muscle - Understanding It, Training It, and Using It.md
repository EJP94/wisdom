> **TL;DR** — The male sex muscle, the root, can squeeze to concentrate and direct energy or push to diffuse it; train it daily for strength, control and balance, and use it through four techniques: the cool, warm and big draws and the root hold.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 275, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- The episode ties together earlier topics (Kegels, moving energy, multiple orgasms without ejaculation, lasting longer) as a foundation for newcomers and a fresh angle for men further along. Probably a good one to share
- **Why it matters.** The male sex muscle is the root, the root chakra covered a few episodes earlier. Strengthening it and learning to use it gives:
  - Better physical energy and foundational energetic support
  - Better erections and better sexual experiences overall
  - Lasting longer
  - Building and moving energy, controlling its flow and transmuting it into other kinds of energy
  - An effective semen retention practice that still lets you fully enjoy sex
- **Where it is.** Seen from the side, a hammock-shaped base of muscle runs from the base of the penis and scrotum to behind the anus, at the very base of the spine. It is several muscles, but their names don't matter, just as you use your arm without knowing its muscles. Treat it as one, because that is how it is engaged, felt and largely how it works
- **Two moves**, renamed from Kegel and reverse Kegel, at least for this episode and maybe going forward, because the new names seem more descriptive and more useful:
  - **Root squeeze** (Kegel): shorten, tighten, contract and lift the hammock along its whole length. It is stopping your pee midstream, or kind of like holding in a fart
  - **Root push** (reverse Kegel): lower, lengthen and extend the hammock, down and out. It is peeing harder, faster or farther, or pushing out a fart
  - Many men struggle to tell a Kegel from a reverse Kegel. Picturing the hammock helps
  - If you're new, a suggestion: for a week or ten days maybe, stop two or three times midstream every time you pee, to learn how to engage the muscle
- You can engage mainly the rear part (around the anus), mainly the front, or the whole thing, and that changes how energy flows. In general engage the whole thing, especially when training. With more strength you can also train the front and rear separately, and you might find you can work with that a little during sex or masturbation
- **How it works:** in two ways
  - **Squeeze side:** concentrates energy, sharpens and brightens it "like a bright ray of sunshine through a window", then directs it. Picture an orgasm: in a lot of ways, energy gathers from the rest of the body, concentrates there and gets directed. By default it is directed out of the body through ejaculation. It can instead direct it up the spine and keep it in the body: the basis of moving energy and multiple orgasms without ejaculation
  - The squeeze generally raises arousal, which seems to be why some men with erection trouble sometimes try to engage it. But drawing back hard on the squeeze side can also kind of withdraw a little blood. So with erection trouble, you may want to practice not engaging it much and find relaxation instead, which lets more blood in
  - **Push side:** diffuses energy, spreads it out and lets it flow through the rest of the body, a smoother "washing over" feeling instead of a sharp, bright one. It generally stabilizes or lowers arousal, which makes it key for lasting longer, for example at level 8 or 9 when you don't want to go over the edge
  - Strengthening and engaging the muscle builds overall life energy, and the more you build it the more you can use it both ways
- **Training: three factors**
  - **Strength**
  - **Control:** how fast you can engage each side, how automatically, in different body positions, and how quickly you can switch between squeeze and push
  - **Balance:** a common mistake. When the squeeze side is strong and the push side weak, the muscle works worse, and typically an automatic squeeze kicks you over into orgasm and ejaculation, so you don't last as long as you want. Personally, no case of a push-side imbalance comes to mind, so if you're out of balance it is likely on the squeeze side
- **Training routine**
  - Train every day: what we do every day is what we become
  - Train both sides. If you feel out of balance, you may want to train the push side about twice as much as the squeeze side
  - Use short, medium and long holds on each side. Apps exist, or just do it. For example:
    - Short: hold 1 second, rest 1 second, about 20 reps
    - Medium: hold 5–6 seconds, rest 3–4 seconds, 10–15 reps
    - Long: hold 20–30 seconds, rest 3–4 seconds, 4–6 reps
  - The order of short, medium and long doesn't really matter. Training every length builds the control you need during sex
  - Engage strongly, not just lightly: like any muscle, it gets stronger when you work it hard
  - Volume depends on how fit you want this muscle to be, as with any fitness. Suggested: at least one set (short, medium and long) on each side every day
  - Suggested: always end on the push side so you don't drift toward the squeeze side. One set each: squeeze, then push. Two to one for balance: maybe push, squeeze, push
  - Now and then, practice switching fast: strong squeeze, strong push, strong squeeze. That builds the mind-body connection you'll need during sex
  - Most reps should engage the whole muscle, front to back
- **Using it** during sex or masturbation has two main uses: controlling and stabilizing arousal so you last longer and let energy flow, and building arousal by concentrating and directing energy. As you progress, you can also send energy toward the front or the back
- **Nasal breathing**, mouth closed, makes all four techniques work much better. If you lose track of your breath, you can't keep track of your energy, which takes finer focus
- **Cool draw** (also called testicle breathing): done without stimulation, anywhere, sitting in the car, at your desk or in the grocery store
  - In private, ideally first cup your balls with one hand for 30–60 seconds to warm them and draw energy. A lot of energy flows through the hands, which disciplines like Tai Chi use
  - On the inhale: a strong root squeeze, lifting and drawing energy from front to back, then to the base of the spine and up it
  - On the exhale: relax, or do a slight push
  - Repeat for 5–10 minutes. Tingling and energy moving up the spine to the head, arms or legs is a good sign
  - The channel the energy travels is often blocked at first. With practice it opens gradually, until energy runs the whole **microcosmic orbit**: a loop from the root up to the crown of the head and back down to the root, front and back
- **Warm draw:** the same draw, but during stimulation (edging or sex), before peak arousal, up to about 9 or 9.5 on a 0–10 scale, where 10 is going over the edge into full orgasm
  - Warming your balls first still helps, especially when edging
  - Inhale with a root squeeze, drawing energy back and up, then relax on the exhale
  - Because the squeeze raises arousal, it can take you over the edge. Start far from 10, low on the arousal scale, and work warmer as the channel opens and control grows
  - Concentrate more energy than you can handle and it kicks you into ejaculation. How much you can redirect depends on your connection, control and stability with that energy
  - Clench other big muscles on the inhale squeeze to "solidify the whole system" so it handles more energy: probably butt and thighs above all, also shoulders, arms, fists, feet on the floor. Clenching the jaw works well for some men, but probably skip it with TMJ or jaw tightness. Clenching seems to pull energy and blood away from the root muscle into those muscles
  - Variation: stimulate while relaxed for several breaths of normal breathing, then do one bigger draw on an inhale. This leads toward the big draw
  - Experiment with how much you stimulate during the draw: at first you may want to stop, or ease off, then gradually keep more stimulation going without going over the edge
- **Big draw:** the warm draw at level 10, full orgasm and maximum energy, when redirecting energy up into the body instead of out needs maximum control
  - Orgasm is a range, not a point, though a lot of men look at it as a point. The big draw separates full orgasm from ejaculation, so you can have multiple full-intensity orgasms and keep the energy in your body
  - Just before the point of no return: a strong root squeeze held for a significant time, drawing energy front to back and up the spine
  - At the same time, clench, and ideally pump, the other big muscles: butt, thighs, arms, hands, shoulders, feet, maybe jaw. In ejaculation the root muscle pumps energy and semen out. The big draw uses the same pumping to send the energy up the spine instead
  - With more practice and a stronger root muscle, you may manage the warm and big draws without clenching other muscles. At the start, clenching helps a lot
- The draws depend on practice and on the muscle's strength. The warmer the energy you want to direct, the more strength, control and awareness you need, so start cooler and build up
- **Root hold**, on the push side: a root push held during stimulation (edging or sex), slight to moderate. It lets energy flow through and spread to the whole body instead of building up in the muscle
  - The purpose isn't lasting longer, it's the connection and the feeling. But the result is lasting longer: you stabilize at a high arousal level, say 7, 8 or 9, and keep the stimulation going, even in high-stimulation positions like sex from behind on your knees
  - The spreading energy also feels good and is healing
  - How: nasal breathing, mouth closed, and an inner calm even while there is outer movement and feeling. Meditation helps a lot here
  - Close the jaw, teeth together, tongue on the roof of the mouth. It seems small but makes a big difference: it closes the energy circuit, like closing an electric circuit, so energy flows more easily and you can stay in the hold
  - At first the rising energy will want to kick you into a squeeze and over the edge, because the body habitually concentrates new energy and sends it out in ejaculation. Hang on through that first hump, let the energy flow and dissipate, and you settle in
  - If you've struggled at all to last as long as you want, dialing in the root hold, along with balancing the push side, is "absolutely transformational"
  - There is still a limit, but practice raises it higher and higher, toward lasting as long as you or your partner want
  - Draws also help you last longer, because drawing energy away takes you back down the arousal scale. The hold instead lets you stay at a level, say 9, and keep going
