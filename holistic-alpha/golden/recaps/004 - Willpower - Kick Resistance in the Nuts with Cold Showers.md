> **TL;DR** — Cold is "your warm friend": a daily cold shower, eased into if you have never taken one, lowers stress, keeps you alert all day, deepens breathing and boosts immunity, and above all builds willpower against resistance.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 4, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- "Cold is your warm friend": it is cold, but your inner fire, where willpower and real strength live, is fueled by it. There is a lot of science on what cold exposure does, including immune responses as you adapt. Personally, the biggest thing found with cold is that it takes you to a deeper, central, more powerful place
- We grew up used to being warm and comfortable. Choosing discomfort through cold is one of the most powerful ways to "kick resistance in the nuts"
- **Daily cold shower**, the one practice to start
  - Every day: twice a week "just doesn't do the same thing". Stick with it long enough and you will feel amazing benefits
  - The earlier in the day, the better
  - If you have never done it, start by finishing your shower on cold, then move to fully cold. Maybe a little warm water to wash
  - Build the time in the cold gradually. You can do more than you think: it takes intention and a decision
  - **Story:** after starting cold showers came ice baths, a tank top most of the winter and seeking out cold whenever possible. Strictly cold showers for two to three years now, with no going back, because of the difference it made in life and in how the mind felt
- Benefits:
  - **Less stress.** The cold is a small stress that body and mind adapt to and grow stronger from, like muscles from lifting weights. It trains you to stay in the moment instead of being swept away by stress
  - **Alertness all day,** not just in the shower. There is a lot of evidence for it, from science and from many people
  - **Deeper breathing,** which the cold triggers on its own, with a lasting effect
  - **Immunity.** Studies have found cold showers increase white blood cells
  - **Willpower,** the biggest one. Starting the day with an intentional choice against comfort strengthens you against resistance in every area of life
- Warm, long showers feel good for a moment, but personally, and probably for a lot of people, they leave you drained and tired, like alcohol and other things that leave you worse off. **Story:** that was the experience before switching to cold
- Cold is uncomfortable at first, like working out: temporary discomfort that leads to something stronger. Start with cold showers; ice baths and outdoor exposure are not needed yet. Cold showers seriously kick ass and "will change your life"
