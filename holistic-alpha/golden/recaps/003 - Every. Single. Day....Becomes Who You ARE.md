> **TL;DR** — What you do every single day becomes who you are, for better (compounding change) or worse (a daily fast-food diet that ended in "a disaster zone"), which is why the podcast is daily and any change should be daily and non-negotiable.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 3, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- "We are what we do on a daily basis." Who you are today is the product of what you have done **every single day**, kept up for weeks and months on end. Not of special events, things done three times a week, or something you do for two weeks and then stop
- Any attempt to change (get in shape, eat better, improve a relationship, add or subtract something) that is not built on a daily habit seems destined to fail. A daily habit compounds, and that is how big changes happen
- It works both ways. **Story:** early adulthood was a miserable time. The daily habits: a 7-Eleven chili hot dog for breakfast, fast food for lunch, McDonald's on the way home and, for a while almost every night, a Taco Bell caramel apple empanada topped with ice cream and caramel syrup (no Taco Bell for many years now). The result was "a disaster zone" physically and mentally
- For the most part, we seem to want to add to our lives: more awesome habits, routines, powers, skills, knowledge and experiences. So it seems fundamental to look at what you do every single day
- **Story:** the podcast itself is daily for two reasons: to help listeners build daily habits, and because impacting as many lives as possible takes shifting one's own daily actions first
- **Make it non-negotiable.** If you need to get in shape, work out every day. That doesn't mean the same workout or crushing yourself every day: some days are active recovery. It is not a plan but a decision that this is now part of who you are
- A daily habit stops being something you do and becomes who you are: working out, eating better, talking with your spouse, being a father (personally, nothing matters more), and everything from your kids to your business to your health. That is when you look back and see how different life used to be. In his experience, what leads there from where you are now is every day
- The weekly versions don't work: "Honey, we should sit down once a week and talk", three workouts this week, or attention to your kids on only two days because stress leaves room for no more
