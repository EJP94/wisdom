> **TL;DR** — Porn, bad habits and reactive talk run on compulsion, discipline and the life you want on intention, and the bridge is a brief pause, suggested before any action and practiced imperfectly, which builds awareness even when you still choose the old action.

*Ideas and stories from Holistic Alpha episode 663, as told by host Steven Mathis. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

- **Discipline** is how we create the life we want, including the inner world: joy, peace and focus. It applies to everything, not just workouts or food: every physical choice, which thoughts we engage with, and how we work through our emotions
- Porn is present right now for a lot of men. For others it is further away, with a little worry it may come back, or it feels well behind them. Either way, the bridge applies to everyone
- **Compulsion vs. intention.** In porn we act from compulsion, not intention. Much of daily life also runs on compulsion, even when it's just habit and may not even be bad or negative. Very few minutes of the day are lived from intention. The more moments lived from intention, the better life gets
- Once you truly want porn out of your life, the difference between wanting that and having it is discipline, and discipline comes from intention
- **Reaction** is close to compulsion. Compulsion probably fits porn or video games that suck your life away better. Reaction fits relationships and conversations: responding at once and often emotionally, through trauma and filters, creates bad communication and often repeats the same negative patterns
- The difference between keeping porn and quitting it, between communication that works and doesn't, and between creating what you want and not, is intention
- **The pause-intention bridge.** Picture walking the same trail every day, with a big hole in it. Beside the hole is a narrow ledge with a rope. Notice the hole coming, step onto the ledge on purpose, and you get across with little effort. It only takes awareness of the hole and of the way across
  - The first step is at least lifting your head. Sometimes, looking down at your feet and pulled along, you fall in the hole all of a sudden
  - The hole can stay. The urge for porn, any other compulsion, or the pull to be lazy is okay and doesn't need to go away. You only need to pause long enough to move with intention
  - It is not a one-time fix but a tool you will use every single day
- **Suggested practice:** pause before any action, even for a fraction of a second or a few seconds. No 10-minute meditation needed. Ask: does this align with my intention, with my truth? Doing that over and over is "the secret, if there is one" to quitting porn and to building "absolute discipline"
- "No action" is still an action. Scrolling your phone or playing video games is an action. Even meditation is: choosing to sit down is an action, and moment to moment the action is listening to your inner world with curiosity. We are always taking action
- Look at the habits you want to get rid of. There is probably no pause and no intention behind them, just getting pulled along
- **Don't expect perfection.** You won't pause 100% of the time, or even close ("never gonna happen"), and even if you did, you wouldn't always act on intention. In coaching, many men working on porn reach a stage where they may pause, sometimes for a long time, see what is happening, and still take the action. That is part of the process: the pause also lets you see the action with more awareness, and you can't change what you aren't aware of. You don't need 100%, or even 80%. Just work on it
- **Story:** recorded on a hike, with pauses before arriving, before choosing a direction and in the car before starting to walk. Pause at critical moments and as often as you can to check: am I acting on intention, and what is my intention?
- **In conversations**, pause before you react or speak. Text conversations give even more room, but it works in person and on the phone too. The briefest pause lets you communicate calmly, connected, not reactive, aligned with your truth instead of anger and old stories
- Pause, intention: the bridge from a compulsive, lower-frequency life to a higher-frequency life of intention, discipline and success, whatever that looks like to you, in every layer of life
