## Intensity and hormones
- Intensity probably makes a bigger difference in your hormonal response than anything else, and you can feel it. Rather than an hour or an hour and a half of slow, low cardio, you'd be much better off going really hard for 15 minutes, like sprints with short breaks if you want to run. **Story:** going back and forth between climbing and push-ups, really hard, really fast. The harder you go in a short time, the more hormonal benefit (002)

## 15 minutes, as hard as you can
- For fitness, if you're not in the shape you want, it does not take a lot of time. Set a timer for 15 minutes and go, every day, and you will see changes, without a doubt (019)
  - The number one thing that matters more than anything else for your fitness is intensity: there are lots of ways to feel better and lots of physical tools, but above all else, just get intense (019)
  - Don't make it a big thing of "intense workouts all the time forever". 15 minutes, set a timer, go as hard as you can: "That's your only job forever." The next day, the same again, at home, at the gym, wherever (019)
