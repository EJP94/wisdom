> **In short** — For testosterone, get some weight: a couple of sets of compound, whole-body exercises like squats, a clean or kettlebell swings. To build strength quickly, focus on total daily volume: a set you can do every time, like 10 push-ups, repeated 20 to 50 times through the day, for example every 15 or 30 minutes. Try it today.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## Why get stronger
- We all want to be stronger: it feels good and, especially as men, it brings a different energy to life (005)
- Physical training, for men, about so much more than looks: see [[discomfort]] (010)

## Compound resistance work
- For testosterone, resistance: you've got to get some weight, which doesn't mean lifting heavy all the time, every day, for hours. A couple of sets of some reps with the right activity can have amazing benefits. Use compound exercises with most of your body, especially your lower body: squats, a clean (like a clean and jerk), kettlebell swings, the kettlebell snatch. Whole body, some explosiveness and some weight give the most bang for your buck (002)
- If morning wood is missing, the fitness basics: some kind of exercise every single day, some days active recovery, some really intense: we are meant, as humans but especially as men, to train our body every single day. Especially compound exercises with moderate to heavy weights, involving all your major muscle groups, like kettlebells, deadlifts, squats and clean and jerk: a huge difference in hormone levels, really maybe the most important thing. That and getting your intensity up seem the two most impactful fitness factors (011)

## Total daily volume: how
- Personally, one of the very favorite ways, amazing in how well it works for building strength quickly, is focusing on total daily volume of activity, especially of a couple of simple activities (005)
- With push-ups, personally one of the very favorite things to do (005)
  - Pick a set that pushes you a little but is pretty reasonable, one you can basically do every time, not your absolute max: say 10, if you'd max out at 15, 20 or 25 (005)
  - Do 20, 30, 40 or 50 sets of 10 through the day, starting in the morning, every 15 or 30 minutes, however you want, a set of 10 or two (005)
  - The longer the period, the better, from waking to bedtime if you can. It doesn't necessarily have to be every 15 minutes (005)
- If you've never tried it, try it for a week, or three days, even one or two. If you can do five push-ups in a set, aim for at least a hundred for the day, 20 sets of five as a minimum: over five hours, that's a set every 15 minutes, with a timer (005)
- Other exercises: bodyweight or Hindu squats, really excellent for the lower body (if you've never heard of Hindu squats, they work really well for this), or pull-ups, if you can do a set of at least one or two or three. You can mix it up (005)
- Pick something you can just get down and do. Squats or push-ups are personally favored because they work anywhere: 10 push-ups take maybe 20 seconds, in the lunchroom at work or at home. After work, 5 to 10 p.m. works too, if you're really diligent with a timer every 15 minutes (005)
- Every day has 96 15-minute increments. Use even half or a quarter of them for one set of something each, and it's amazing how quickly you get stronger (005)
- The bottom line: lots of sets through the day, targeting a big total daily volume number, 20 to 50 sets of whatever number you can do, varying the hours and the minutes in between. Make each set hard enough to kind of push you while you can still do it every time. By the 40th or 50th set, if you do that many, they obviously get harder. You'll probably be amazed at the results (005)
- If it's early enough in the day, try it today: there's such power in today, so don't let it be a tomorrow thing. Even late, kick it off today, then try it tomorrow. It costs one day of effort, and in reality you'll lose nothing and gain a ton. Push-ups, pull-ups and squats all work great, and the recommendation is push-ups, with a high number for the day (005)

## Total daily volume: why it works
- Each set activates those muscles and seems to give them a little signal to grow. A full workout of half an hour to two hours needs at least until the next day to recover. Mini sets aren't intense and don't really break you down: they just say "keep growing", and your body kind of heals a little and grows a little. At least that's the way it seems to work, experientially (005)
- Total volume builds really quickly this way, and you'll probably surprise yourself with how much you can do and how fast strength grows (005)
- If you've never tried it, you'll be amazed how quickly you get stronger. It also seems an awesome way to set a new sort of record for yourself, and it adjusts your perspective (005)
- **Story:** never having really done this, or that many push-ups, the goal was a thousand push-ups a day for probably about a week, not reached every day. Surely some guys can do way more, and probably a lot of people can't do one, so the number doesn't matter. What mattered was that a thousand in one workout seemed completely unreachable, yet broken into little chunks through the day, it adds up powerfully (005)
- Push-ups are such a basic, functional strength thing that everybody seems able to benefit from more of them, whatever your sport or however you want to improve your body or fitness (005)
