> **In short** — Sprinting is one of the very best ways to boost your hormonal profile: studies show as little as one six-second, maximum-effort sprint raised growth hormone for as much as 60 minutes. If you're not sprinting now, the recommendation: five six-second sprints, two or three times a week, working up to 30 seconds, with a suggested warm-up. Go absolutely all out.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## Why sprint
- Sprinting is one of the very best ways to boost your hormonal profile as a man (023)
- Think primal: for optimizing hormones especially, it really helps, since much of our body and mind responds the way it would have in a primal time, not necessarily in today's world. As cavemen, sprinting meant chasing down a hunt or running from a predator, and our bodies got the message "I need to be a man right now": strength, power and the hormones to kill this bison or run away from this bear. So it just makes sense that sprinting at absolute maximum effort signals our hormones to boost, and they really do: modern science shows it in the numbers we can test (023)
- It's one of the most beneficial things you can add to your life, even with no other changes to your fitness, nutrition or anything else: just add it two or three times a week. It's amazing how little time it takes, so there's absolutely no excuse of not having time: you either put in the effort or you don't (023)
- Studies show as little as one six-second sprint of maximum effort raised human growth hormone levels for as much as 60 minutes after, and boosts testosterone as well. Not a lot of time, just the effort, and you'll really feel the benefits in your hormones, "I promise you" (023)
- Other benefits: it raises your metabolism, helps burn fat, and brings cardiovascular benefits. But the big reason all men should really be sprinting, it seems, is that it boosts hormones in really powerful ways, independent of any other changes. If you're already kind of kicking ass with fitness, nutrition and lifestyle, with hormones in great shape, you'll get an even further boost, feel even stronger and have even more human growth hormone and testosterone. Conversely, if you're not in a great place hormonally, for any number of reasons, just starting with sprinting will really give you a boost in getting your hormones back into a good place (023)
- Psychologically too: if it's been a while since you really sprinted, doing five, even six-second ones, brings an amazing mental and mood boost along with the hormonal boost. There's no damn excuse not to: literally a couple of minutes of effort, but you have to go hardcore (023)

## How to sprint
- How to do it (023)
  - Start with individual sprints of at least six seconds. The recommendation if you're not sprinting now: five sprints of six seconds, 30 seconds in total, with, say, a 5 or 10 second break between. Two minutes or less, and you can make incredible changes in your testosterone and human growth hormone levels. Two or three times a week, "That's all it takes": less than 10 minutes a week for all the sprint workouts you need (023)
  - Work up gradually in sprint length, eventually to maybe five 30-second sprints: still only two and a half minutes of running and maybe as much break, five minutes or less a couple of times a week, a pretty good routine with amazing results (023)
  - The studies measured one six-second and one 30-second sprint. The six-second sprint raised both human growth hormone and testosterone, for at least 60 minutes after. The 30-second sprint raised human growth hormone by 450% more than the six-second one, and kept it elevated 90 to 120 minutes instead of 60. So definitely work up to sprints as long as 30 seconds. Beyond 30 seconds you're more than likely not really in a full all-out sprint, it seems, "Or if you are, then man, good on you" (023)
  - Ideally you're outside, running. The personal favorite, and seemingly the ideal way: barefoot when possible, in a park, on the grass, which seems to put us in a solid biomechanical place with our running (023)
  - In a shit ton of snow, or stuck in a hotel in the middle of the airport, an elliptical is a good backup: you can recreate sprinting pretty well on one. A little caution with a treadmill: you could try it, but absolute full speed, all-out on one seems a little sketchy. But ideally get outside, even for two minutes, even one (023)
  - Go hardcore, absolutely all out, not a 60%, kind-of-running-fast (023)
  - Depending on your fitness and flexibility, a few minutes to kind of warm up are suggested: some burpees, push-ups, jumping jacks, squats, or rolling out your legs with a massage roller. Be smart and don't pull a hamstring: walking out of the office on your lunch break after sitting all day and instantly bolting into a full hundred percent sprint is not recommended. Give at least a couple of minutes, depending on your condition, to loosen up the muscles (023)
