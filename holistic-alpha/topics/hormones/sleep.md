## Sleep and testosterone
- For testosterone, sleep is absolutely crucial. Most hormone production happens during sleep, and too little sleep raises cortisol and stress hormones, which really don't coexist with testosterone: more stress hormones generally means lower testosterone. Enough sleep gives double the benefit, too little hurts both ways (002)
