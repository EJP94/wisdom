## DHEA
- One really important hormone, among those meditation improves, is DHEA, also referred to as kind of the grandmother hormone. It acts on its own, but also produces lots of downstream hormones, including testosterone for men and estrogen for women (020)
- DHEA is really closely linked to aging: levels gradually decline as we age, and doctors use it as one of the markers of sort of the true age of your body, versus your birth certificate. Higher DHEA, more youth (020)
- Meditators' DHEA levels: see [[meditation]] (020)
- More DHEA not only increases youth, it also tends to increase sex drive, because it's converted into downstream hormones like testosterone (020)

## Endorphins
- A lot of people know endorphins are released with exercise, as in a runner's high, and make us feel incredibly good. They also lead to a lot of sort of mind and physical state conducive to a positive sex drive and sex experience (020)
- Meditation and endorphins: see [[meditation]] (020)
- Endorphins make you feel awesome, put you more in the mood for sex and more able to enjoy it, with really powerful physiological effects on your body and mental and physical state (020)
