## Stress and testosterone
- Keep stress down, since high cortisol impacts testosterone. Sleep, meditation, exercise and a few supplements help. Personally, the favorite is ashwagandha (002)

## Stress and sex drive
- Stress puts the body into fight or flight mode, where things like sex drive are basically shut down and take a back seat to surviving: the state cavemen were in defending against an animal. Today it's work, relationships, whatever stress weighs on us. The more stress, the more sex drive feels the effects (020)
- To the extent we reduce stress, we improve sex drive: it's just the way our bodies work, a direct inverse relationship. Less stress, and the more you feel like having sex, the better you focus, the more present you are, the better you perform and the more you enjoy it. Better for life, and certainly for your sex life (020)
