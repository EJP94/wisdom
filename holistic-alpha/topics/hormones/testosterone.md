## Why hormones matter
- Hormones are super important. As they say, "hormones affect everything and everything affects your hormones", and it's so true: how you feel, perceive, act and think, your confidence, your mental and physical well-being. Optimizing them and your testosterone puts you in the strongest position to beat resistance and get what you desire (001)

## The 80/20 of testosterone
- The 80/20 principle basically means that 80% of the benefit or results come from 20% of the activity, in everything from personal life to business to milk production in Wisconsin. The book *80/20 Sales and Marketing* by Perry Marshall teaches a lot more about it (002)
- For testosterone, the 20% that gives 80%, the "most bang for your hormonal buck", is four areas with three keys each, in no particular order, since where you get the most depends on your particular circumstance (002)
  - Lifestyle, three keys: sleep, low stress and removing toxins. See [[sleep]], [[stress]], [[toxins]] (002)
  - Nutrition: magnesium matters, with pumpkin seeds a great source, and cruciferous vegetables like broccoli and cabbage remove excess estrogens, but it seems to boil down to three keys: good fats, no toxic fats, no sugar. See [[fats-sugar]] (002)
  - Sex absolutely impacts your testosterone and hormones in major ways. Three keys: less ejaculation, rarely if ever alone, an active sex life. See [[semen-retention]] (002)
  - Fitness, three keys: daily activity, intensity and compound resistance work. See [[intensity]], [[strength]] (002)
    - Daily activity: you've got to move every day. Hormones are your body's response to its environment: run into a bear on a trail and it spikes adrenaline and cortisol, both hormones. That's largely what hormones really are, in a lot of ways your body's signal and response mechanism. Just sitting signals that you don't really need testosterone. Daily activity signals that you need to be strong and active, so you need testosterone and not too much estrogen (002)
  - The best way to boost testosterone is the 20% of things that bring 80% of the results, the fundamentals: sleep, nutrition, the right kind of exercise, and conserving your sex energy instead of wasting it with excess ejaculation. They make by far the biggest difference, raising low levels and boosting them more. Supplements are still an awesome way to boost sexual function and life force (016)
- Each area holds a lot more detail, and where you get the very most bang for your buck may depend on your specific circumstances (002)
