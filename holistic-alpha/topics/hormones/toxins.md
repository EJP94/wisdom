## Glutathione
- Glutathione, one of our body's most powerful antioxidants, can be impacted negatively by poor nutrition, toxins and stress, and levels decline with age. In general, the more we have, the healthier and better off we are. A lot of athletes, and other people, supplement with it, and it's quite expensive (009)
- During a coffee enema, glutathione in your liver spikes by somewhere in the neighborhood of 700%, according to studies. It's one of the most powerful compounds in your body, and people pay good money to supplement it externally, but, just like any other supplement, taken externally it isn't absorbed the same way as when our body makes it. It's so important for cellular function and ridding the body of toxins, and really powerful in how it can make us feel (009)
