## What it is
- A lot of us associate dopamine with different things than it really is. It is super important for our life drive and our desire to get shit done; when it's low, that really suffers, as do mood and energy (016)

## What affects it
- Porn and your dopamine: see [[_parked-sex-energy#Porn]] (006)
- Porn, too much TV and too much ejaculation, especially masturbating, crush dopamine levels; smoking, drinking and lots of things have a negative effect on the levels and the whole dopamine system, receptors included. The effects add up, and low dopamine can creep up on you over time (016)
