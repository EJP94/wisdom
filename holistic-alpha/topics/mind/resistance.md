> **In short** — Resistance is an opposing force inside you, not other people, whose one purpose is to push back; it never dies. Beating it seems to come down to strengthening who you are from the inside out: physical well-being, hormones, fasting, sexual energy and the mind, getting after each area every day. Being an alpha male is about dominating resistance within you.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## What it is
- The universe is governed by a law of duality, of opposing forces, everywhere and in everything: opposite the light is dark. They don't cancel each other out but balance each other out, within us and our lives too (001)
- We have a drive, a power that wants to carry us towards our dreams and who we're supposed to be. Opposite that good inside you is an opposing force, resistance, as it's called here (001)
  - Not other people, not anything in your physical world: it lives inside you, living and breathing every day as much as you are (001)
  - Its one purpose is to push back: to hold you back from where you want to go and who you want to be, to make you stop instead of putting in the effort, to keep you quiet when you should speak up in a relationship (001)
  - It will be with you every day, in every aspect of your life. You cannot kill it: resistance never dies (001)

## The thoughts it plants
- You kick ass, something we don't always hear enough. Sometimes resistance sticks thoughts in our head like "oh, you're not good enough", weak, not smart enough. Screw that (009)
- Resistance's thoughts against trying coffee enemas: see [[toxins]] (009)

## How to beat it
- Resistance seems to show up in every single aspect of life, so beating it is not about those individual aspects but about stepping back and changing, evaluating and strengthening who you are as a man from the inside out (001)
- Get stronger in all the ways resistance can't handle, so you face it every day from strength, as "this big, confident, badass", and it comes cowering (001)
- You probably have areas, as is personally the case too, where you're "really kicking his ass", and others where it hands you yours. The more areas you beat it in, the more it gets the message, and the easier it gets (001)
- Beating resistance seems to come down to a few things (001)
  - Physical well-being is absolutely critical: your body affects your mind, emotions, relationships and success in business. You can't attack resistance from your greatest strength without taking care of it, and that starts with fitness, nutrition, the right supplements and optimizing your hormones. When resistance beats you physically, it's like the first battle of the day is already lost, and you go into every other one weaker (001)
  - Hormones: see [[testosterone]] (001)
  - Fasting: see [[fasting]] (001)
  - Sexual energy: see [[life-force]] (001)
  - The mind. However strong your body and sexual power, if you don't understand your mind and aren't willing to work with it positively, none of the rest matters. It doesn't take a lot: attitude and mindset, working out in a way that builds the mind's strength for tough situations, and a willingness to sit still and meditate for a few minutes (001)
- None of this is honestly complicated, but that doesn't mean it's easy. Get after each area every day (001)
- Cold showers, against resistance and for willpower: see [[_parked-mind#Cold exposure]] (004)
- Physical training as facing resistance in an arena of your choosing: see [[discomfort]] (010)
- Out of alignment, resistance within us gains incredible power over us. Brave enough to change and live in a way that feels true and right to you, regardless of what anybody thinks, you flip the tables and gain incredible power over him (017)
- The more true joy lies behind something, most often the more resistance you'll face getting to it. Either we accept facing resistance over and over and move towards that thing anyway, or we let it stand in the way and die with regret. Be brave (021)
- **Story:** years, in so many different areas, were spent unhappy from not being willing to face resistance and pursue happiness (021)

- We're not wolves, we're human beings. Being an alpha male is not about dominating other people but about dominating resistance within you (001)
