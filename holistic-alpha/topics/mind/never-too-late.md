> **In short** — You can absolutely be the strongest, best version of you at 30 or 40 or 50 or 60: going from out of shape and lost to stronger and healthier than ever is not something special about one man. Not being stuck in your job, career or situation is parked under work.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## Not stuck

## At 30, 40, 50 or 60
- Going from being out of shape and lost to stronger and healthier than ever is not something special about one man: we can all do that. You can absolutely be the strongest, best version of you at 30 or 40 or 50 or 60.
