> **In short** — Living your truth is the only way to access your real power; basing choices on what others think is like driving with your foot on the brake. Think about what is maybe out of alignment, and decide today. Happiness, yours and what you bring others, is the ultimate measure, and knocking out what isn't contributing to it sometimes takes bravery.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## Living your truth
- Living your truth, whatever that means to you, is the only way to access your real abilities, willpower, strength and creativity. We can't live somebody else's truth: we don't even know it, though too often we guess it (017)
- Beyond happiness, your ability to create, build, grow and progress is directly linked with your alignment with who you truly are: the more you discover and follow it, the greater the power (017)
- Love is what it's all about: not about not loving others, but about loving yourself enough to do what you know is right for you (017)

## What others think
- Too often we base so much of our choices on what someone else thinks, or what we think they'll think: a spouse, parents, kids, friends, coworkers. That path leads not just to less happiness but to less power (017)
- It's like driving with your foot on the gas and the brake at the same time: basically nowhere, on a real rough ride. Living what's true for you individually is the only way to get your foot off the brake; on the gas, looking ahead, you make a hell of a lot more progress (017)
- Too often we give too many fucks away to other people. Stop giving a fuck what people think: what do you think, who are you, what do you really want? With one life to live, that's what matters (017)

## Change what's out of alignment
- An encouragement for today: think about what in your life is maybe not in alignment with who you truly are, a job, a relationship your heart isn't in anymore, personal habits and lifestyles not really in alignment with who you truly know you are, friends with a negative impact, and how it's impacting you. Maybe you already know deep down. The moment you align it with who you are and what you desire, your power and speed towards the life you want are truly unleashed; while it stays out of line, you're never going to access your real power (017)
- Out of alignment, resistance gains power over us: see [[resistance]] (017)
- Don't wait for the power, mood or strength to make the change: make it, and your power will grow exponentially (017)
- Make some kind of decision today: everybody can live more in alignment, more of who we are and less of who we think somebody wants us to be. Be what you really want, without waiting for someone to say it's okay, and "you will kick more ass than you can possibly imagine" (017)

## Happiness is the measure
- What matters more than anything is happiness, yours and what you bring others: "that's the only thing that matters", that and making memories. Stuff, being in shape, a good sex life, awesome kids, the trappings of the modern world are nice, but nothing else matters but your happiness (021)
- Happiness is the measure, the ultimate measure of any alpha male, not the stuff, the people you know or the money. Only you know, within your own life and mind, how to be the most happy and bring the most happiness to others (021)
- Really fit but miserable? Who cares. In fitness, a job or a relationship, "quote unquote success" without true happiness on a deep core level is failure, not success (021)

## Long-term happiness takes bravery
- Miserable in a job that pays for your lifestyle, or unhappy in a relationship you're not brave enough to end: is that pursuing happiness in a way that feels true on the deepest level? Maybe not. Knocking out a job, relationship or habit that isn't contributing to our happiness sometimes takes bravery (021)
- Happiness is not easy: long-term happiness is not doing whatever feels good in the moment, and a lot of times it's the opposite. Sleeping in and sitting around all day might feel good in the moment, but leaves you feeling worse and worse the longer you do it; not facing what you need to face in a relationship leaves you more and more unhappy; job hunting or building a business to escape an unhappy job might not feel good, but it's what long-term happiness takes (021)
- Bringing happiness to those we love, our family: see [[partner-family]] (021)
- Resistance in front of true joy: see [[resistance]] (021)
- Pursuing happiness, the way to a life without regret: see [[regret]] (021)
- We all, it seems, have some aspect of our life we know inherently we truly want to change, for a different level of joy and satisfaction for us and maybe those around us, but we let resistance stand in the way. Choose happiness, and go after whatever is eating at you (021)
- You have more resistance-facing power than you've ever felt before. Today, take some positive step in an area of your life where you haven't been: figure out what holds you back from the most happiness and get after changing it (021)

## Choosing it
- Happiness doesn't just happen. We choose to be happy kind of moment to moment, it seems: no matter how bad things are, we can always kind of choose our attitude. Long-term happiness is up to us too: choosing a path that creates happiness for us and others, the only thing we'll take with us (021)
- The 15 minutes talked about a couple of days ago: every day, 15 minutes toward what will bring the greatest increase in joy for you and those around you, like submitting job applications, planning and building your own business, or setting a timer to talk and figure stuff out in a relationship (021)
