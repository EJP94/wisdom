> **In short** — Any fundamental change seems to start with every single day: what you do daily for weeks and months becomes who you are, for better or worse, and compounds really powerfully. Make it every day and non-negotiable, not three times a week. Stop comparing yourself to anybody else: focus on being better today than yesterday.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## You are what you do every day
- Any fundamental change seems to start with every single day. You are what you do daily, not three times a week, not for two weeks and then stop. What you do every single day for weeks and months literally becomes who you are, not the special events (003)
  - "We are the things that we do every day." It doesn't take a lot of time, but it takes an intention. A negative activity probably happens at the same time every day: when it's about to start, set a timer and just don't do it for 15 minutes (019)
- So to change, improve, add or even subtract something, it's got to be every day. Any attempt to get in shape, eat better or improve a relationship not focused on every day seems destined to fail (003)
- Conversely, what you do every single day, not twice a week or twice a month, compounds really powerfully. That's how tremendous changes happen (003)
- It works against you as much as for you. **Story:** early in adulthood, absolutely miserable, the daily eating was something like a 7-Eleven hot dog with chili for breakfast and fast food for lunch and on the way home. For a while, almost nightly, literally every single day, came McDonald's and a Taco Bell caramel apple empanada, "this frozen taco thing from hell", with ice cream and caramel syrup added. No Taco Bell in many years now, thank goodness. The result: a disaster zone, physically and mentally (003)
- For the most part we seem to want to add things: habits, routines, powers, skills, knowledge, experiences. So it seems fundamental to look at what you do every single day (003)

## How to make it every day
- Whatever you want to change, start with every single day, and don't make it negotiable. To get in shape, work out every single day: not the same thing, not crushing yourself nonstop, some days active recovery or whatever. Not three times a week, not a plan, but: "this hasn't been a part of every single day who I am. But going forward, it's gonna be a part of every single day who I am" (003)
- Not "hey honey, we should sit down once a week and talk", not three workouts this week, not attention to your kids two days this week because stress lets you focus only on those two. Not three, two or five times a week. Make it every day, non-negotiable, and you will see tremendous change and impact (003)

## What changes
- Made every day, it becomes who you are instead of something that you do, from working out, eating and your relationship to being a father, your kids, your business, your health. Personally, nothing in life is more important than being a father. Communicating with your spouse, or caring for your body, goes from a few times a week to part of who you are (003)
- That's when the really powerful changes happen: you look back and see how dramatically different that part of life used to be. From experience, personally, what leads there is every day (003)

## Better than yesterday
- Stop comparing your body, business or relationships to anybody else's. Focus on being better today than yesterday, personally the whole aim (001)

## 15 minutes at a time
- Every 24-hour period holds 96 15-minute blocks, and 1/96th of the day can change your life, and is changing it, for better or for worse. Too often we look at the big, huge task and everything it will take, and let that stop us from doing the thing: repairing a relationship, getting fit, eating better, building a business, being a better father. Mentally trying to reach the place of having accomplished the whole thing makes it seem huge (019)
- That's not the best way to look at it, and not how it works: we live life a moment at a time, really, not a project at a time. But we need some sort of period we can attack and really make changes in: 15 minutes, or thereabouts (019)
- In 15 minutes you might work your body (every day, and you're way more fit), drive to the liquor store for beer, have real communication with your spouse, or get lost on social media. The really good, powerful things can be knocked out 15 minutes at a time, at least, and what negatively affects us is those short periods too, sometimes the ones that don't happen, like 15 minutes of real communication with your spouse every day. That's where all of the change lies, where your power lies (019)
- A strong belief in being present, and in meditating to practice presence, since that seems to be where we're most effective and also most joyful: a moment-by-moment thing. But planning or eliminating an activity, creating or eliminating a habit, takes a little more than a moment. "15 minutes you can change your life. 15 minutes you are changing your life" (019)
- Set a timer: if your relationship with your spouse could use improving, set one in the other room, and when it goes off, immediately walk over and talk for 15 minutes, not forcing conversation if it's not the right time. It doesn't have to be six hours, or the whole project, or getting all the way fit, building the whole business or fixing the whole relationship at once. 15 minutes at a time, every day, will change your life in more powerful ways than you can possibly imagine. For a relationship, the most important thing is probably communicating, planned every day: call that person, or talk to your spouse about what has kind of come up in the last few days, or "I've been feeling this, or I see maybe you're feeling that". Real communication will do amazing things (019)
- It doesn't take a lot: we have to break stuff into smaller chunks so we can take action, both to add awesomeness to your life and to get rid of things (019)
- Focus on something for 15 minutes. "It's 1% of the day." Everything will change (019)
