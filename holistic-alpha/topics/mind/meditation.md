## Meditation and sex energy
- To move towards separating orgasm and ejaculation and gain control, practice meditation, which might sound unrelated but is incredibly related. Moving through five to nine on the arousal scale without going over the edge to 10, where your sex energy crashes, is so linked to your mind: it's not just physical. Controlling your breathing and harnessing your thoughts make a huge difference (006)
- Meditation has a direct and powerful link not only to your physical state and sex drive, but also to your performance and enjoyment of sex itself, in four ways (020)
- Stress: meditation destroys stress, and stress destroys sex drive (020)
  - How stress shuts sex drive down: see [[stress]] (020)
- Hormones: tremendous physical benefits on a hormonal level. Less stress means less cortisol and related stress hormones, and meditation also improves the hormones we want (020)
  - DHEA, kind of the grandmother hormone: see [[_parked-hormones#DHEA]] (020)
  - Meditators were studied and found to have higher DHEA levels than non-meditators by as much as 40%: a huge, drastic impact on actual hormone levels. It's not just about a clear mind, concentration or presence: there are really dramatic physical benefits to mind and body (020)
- Endorphins: a lot of people don't really associate them with meditation (020)
  - What endorphins do: see [[_parked-hormones#Endorphins]] (020)
  - Meditation boosts them too: one study in the Biological Psychology Journal in 1995, "so it's been a while", studied elite runners and meditators and found very similar endorphin-boosting effects (020)
- Presence: without question, meditation helps your ability to be present, with a focused, quiet mind (020)
  - One of the other big things that kills sex drive, and especially your sex life and ability to perform and enjoy, is your ability to be present. If you're mentally distracted and your mind is always somewhere else, you're never going to be in the moment anywhere near the same way. It affects performance and enjoyment, and when constant throughout the day, your sex drive too (020)
  - The more you get comfortable with your mind and step back, not caught up in all the clouds running around but chilling in the blue sky above them, the more you can be in the moment with sex, your sex energy and your sex life (020)
  - With a more focused and quiet mind you're simply a better lover: you last longer, you're more attentive, you enjoy sex better and you're more able to control your arousal level (020)
- Meditation has really powerful benefits in so many aspects of life, and one of them is most definitely your sex life (020)
