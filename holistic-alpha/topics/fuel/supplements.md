## For energy: the top five
- **Story:** the last decade went into taking dozens and dozens of supplements and researching, a lot of it around energy, since a lot of us are looking for more energy in our lives. Back in the day: energy drinks, and feeling absolutely horrible from them; years of mistakes and dialing things in followed (013)
- The personal top five for real, healthy energy, in no particular order, or maybe in a particular order in this case: coffee, acetyl-L-carnitine, alpha GPC, cordyceps and shilajit (013)
- The top five found in all the research and experimentation, with an invitation to try any or all: "They're all safe and awesome and I think would benefit you" (013)

## Coffee
- Coffee, amazing for energy, in a healthy way, and for hell's sake not soda: a morning can of soda is a completely different thing, and so is tea (013)
- For years a lot of people looked at coffee as maybe a vice, but more and more science has come to show it's very, very good for us, with a number of really well-proven benefits beyond energy (013)
- More mental and physical energy and a faster brain, at a moderate amount: a couple cups, not eight shots of espresso. A study shows a couple cups can cut post-workout muscle pain by up to 48% (013)
- Some of the other scientifically proven benefits: associated with a lower risk of diabetes, Alzheimer's, MS and colorectal cancer; a really large study shows it reduces suicide risk and depression; studies show it protects against Parkinson's; coffee drinkers have shown in studies a lower risk of heart disease, and DNA of stronger integrity (013)
- It can make you feel great and get more done, and seems to have a place in every adult's life, and probably a lot of teenagers could benefit from a moderate amount. In your life it most definitely has a place: a huge source of energy beyond the basics (013)

## Acetyl-L-carnitine
- Acetyl-L-carnitine, maybe not heard of, like the rest of the list: an amino acid; carnitine is produced naturally in our body (013)
- Its big role is with our mitochondria, absolutely crucial to so much of our health, wellness and energy, with a lot of science around them now. On a cellular level they have a huge role in helping our body run effectively, so acetyl-L-carnitine, by essentially boosting them, basically boosts one of the most important aspects of virtually every cell. Almost every cell has them, and one of their major roles is energy production, so boosting them boosts energy on a cellular level (013)
  - Acetyl-L-carnitine, covered in an episode on energy, absolutely improves energy, but most definitely the brain too. Basically a mitochondrial-boosting supplement: mitochondria, essentially the powerhouse of our cells, are in almost every cell and especially dense in brain cells, so boosting them boosts energy overall and in the brain (022)
- Of the forms of carnitine, it crosses the blood-brain barrier into the brain's mitochondria, a dense source of them and a really important area to fuel: it impacts both mental and physical energy directly (013)
  - Carnitine is in all our cells, and you can buy just L-carnitine: look for the acetyl-L-carnitine form, which crosses the blood-brain barrier to act on the brain's mitochondria, improving cognition, learning, memory and mood (022)
- It ramps up energy production, increases endurance and boosts the mitochondria's ability to burn fat, both for losing fat and for using fat as fuel, "which is the most effective fuel for our body" (013)
  - Acting on mitochondria everywhere, it also reduces fatigue, shows notable improvements in sperm quality in the studies (obviously your balls have mitochondria too) and improves muscular endurance: really wide-ranging benefits. A favorite, taken on a regular basis (022)
- Absolutely one of the personal favorites, relatively inexpensive: personally, one to really try not to be without, other than just occasionally cycling off. Used by our body at such a fundamental level, it seems to have a long-term place in our lives (013)
- Fasting's mitochondria boost: see [[fasting]] (022)

## Alpha GPC
- Alpha GPC, a source of choline, in simple terms essentially fuel in our brain, and critical: a really important nutrient we need. It comes from diet, like eggs, but a lot of us are deficient, and supplementing has some amazing benefits (013)
  - Alpha GPC, alpha glycerophosphocholine, kind of the sister supplement to Huperzine A. Huperzine A improves choline levels in the brain, and actually the body, by inhibiting its breakdown; Alpha GPC is a direct source of choline, which turns into acetylcholine, kind of the downstream form, the actual neurotransmitter. The combination is really powerful for brain and body (022)
- Brain and body are so connected, and there's a lot of science around things like increased power output. Personally, a tremendous physical impact in addition to fueling the brain: a tremendous overall energy boost, in a healthy way (013)
  - A lot of studies show improved power output and physical performance in athletes. Exercise drops choline levels, and Alpha GPC prevents those exercise-induced reductions. Taking choline prior to a workout, the power output difference personally felt is amazing; you'll feel it in your brain the rest of the day, and certainly in your workout (022)
- Of the forms, such as CDP choline, alpha GPC has personally been the best (013)
- Timing: it tends to take about two hours for maximum benefits, so take it a couple hours before your workout. The other four of the top five take effect within about 15 to 30 minutes, once digested, a great window pre-workout. **Story:** all or most of these, depending on what is being short-term cycled off, make the pre-workout mix on an almost daily basis (013)
  - It takes a little longer to take effect as a pre-workout: take it a good hour and a half or two hours before the workout, and have your coffee or whatever, say, 15 minutes before, since those work a lot more quickly (022)
- Like lots of neurotransmitters, choline isn't just used in the brain but throughout the body, with a lot of benefits for both (022)
- For Alpha GPC, quite a few good brands; a favorite is the NOW brand, available all over and taken on a regular basis, along with others (022)
- It's isolated from a food, from a couple of sources including soy. Not a big fan of soy, but it's such an isolated substance, without soy proteins, that it seems not a big deal (022)
- The preferred dosage of most of the studies is 600 milligrams. Personally, 600 milligrams usually twice a day; maybe once a day might be good for you, depending on your volume of physical activity. When that's pretty high, twice a day before your workouts seems really the ideal time (022)

## Cordyceps
- Cordyceps, a mushroom. Of the ton of beneficial mushrooms, like reishi and lion's mane, it's really the most well known for physical sort of power and energy (013)
- Personally, nothing gets taken or recommended now without science behind it. Cordyceps is a really, truly ancient medicine, used by humans for a really long time, with a lot of modern science too (013)
- Unlike alpha GPC, a very specific nutrient, it has a little more wide-ranging impacts, as adaptogens and mushrooms benefit the body in multiple ways. A main one is energy on a fundamental, cellular kind of level: it has been shown very effective at boosting ATP, a molecule really essential for delivering energy to our body. Lots of studies show VO2 max, aerobic ability, improving with it, and there are studies on anti-aging and anti-cancer properties (013)
- Personally, truly increased physical energy and endurance: doing more and feeling better, day after day, on it than off it (013)
- Another favorite: it really increases lung function noticeably, with some science, and most definitely noticed personally: breathing is naturally deeper and stronger, sometimes striking when restarting after cycling off. For any breathing, lung or congestion issue, cordyceps is amazing (013)
- Cordyceps, a mushroom, also amazing for energy, as covered in the episode on energy supplements. One of the personal top three favorite supplements ever found for sex energy, working in a fundamental way: adaptogenic, it affects the body along a number of pathways of sexual function (016)
- Personally felt, and shown by some of the science: really improved drive and sex energy, like the difference between having just ejaculated and having that sex energy built up inside: "It's like the only thing that I've ever really found that just kind of boosts that energy" (016)
- As you really find connecting with people online who have tried it, it increases stamina, sex performance and how long you last in really significant ways, which every man wants: absolutely worth getting (016)
- A number of companies make good quality extracts; the personal favorite is Host Defense (016)

## Shilajit
- Shilajit, very likely not heard of: an almost tar-like, sticky substance that squeezes out of rocks in the mountains, primarily the Himalayas, basically really, really old plant material condensed and treated by the earth over time (013)
- Highly nutrient dense, with 80-plus minerals, so you only need a really, really small amount, and fulvic acid, of which it is pretty much the best source: an antioxidant that helps the body in a number of ways, including getting rid of toxins, fighting off reactions that cause allergy symptoms, reducing inflammation and preventing the growth of cancer, and it stimulates the immune system: it does lots of things in our body (013)
- Probably different from anything you've ever taken, and maybe the most powerful supplement personally taken in terms of truly feeling it: within a day, or a moderate amount the first time, it's really powerful for overall energy (013)
- A lot of science on it: it benefits and reduces Alzheimer's disease, improves low testosterone and chronic fatigue, is used against high altitude sickness, helps fight iron deficiencies, improves fertility and heart health. Personally, powerful energy benefits: highly suggested (013)

Definitely look for quality cordyceps: there are a number of good quality companies, so pay attention that you actually buy one of those. For alpha GPC, acetyl-L-carnitine and coffee, pretty much most companies are relatively good, though look for organic coffee when you can. With shilajit, spend some extra time: there's a lot of lower quality out there, especially blends that supposedly include it, which often have very poor quality or not much of it (013)
- Sex power supplements are a huge market, frankly full of lots of really nasty shit to avoid: pretty much any blended product purporting some sort of sexual function, personally, just avoid. **Story:** years of experimenting with so much: individual herbs, supplements and all kinds of blends (016)
- Avoid blends: unless they come from the very, very few makers of trusted, high quality supplements from the ground up, really hard to find in this niche, most seem really scary, for two reasons (016)
  - All kinds of ingredients, and we don't know how we react to which one: true of any blend, and why blends were left behind over the years (016)
  - A lot of sex power blends are full of unlisted, low quality ingredients, in some cases kind of fraudulent about what's in them: nasty, scary stuff (016)
- The personal practice now: individual supplements and herbs, to test, most of the time a better, safer way, especially for sex power (016)

## Mucuna pruriens
- Mucuna pruriens, the dopa bean or velvet bean, a natural source of dopamine. A prescription drug, Levodopa, is pure, a hundred percent dopamine; mucuna, depending on the harvest, is around 15% dopamine, with lots of other beneficial compounds: in a lot of ways more effective, but definitely safer (016)
- **Story:** the first time on mucuna was shocking: for the first time, the difference between proper and decreased dopamine levels could be felt. It had gradually gotten worse, and it was amazing to realize: that's what it was, not enough dopamine (016)
- Not enough dopamine is probably a really widespread problem, among a lot of men too: very likely you'd feel significant benefits (016)
- A widely available brand, personally tested a lot: NOW, usually pretty good, its mucuna definitely; search NOW mucuna pruriens or NOW Dopa, as the label probably says Dopa Mucuna (016)
- It has been shown to increase testosterone, not the focus, as lifestyle seems to matter more. Definitely found, and shown by a lot of the studies around mucuna: dopamine in a good place benefits your drive to get stuff done and your confidence with people and with women, affected big time (016)
- It definitely boosts the personal desire for sex and libido, along the pathways of sort of an increased confidence and desire for life, an edge probably none of us would mind (016)

## Maca
- Maca, a root kind of like a turnip, really a food we now kind of take as a supplement. It comes largely from Peru, where they cook and eat it as a vegetable (016)
- Awesome for balancing hormones: not a dramatic testosterone boost, but it absolutely has been shown, in women and especially in men, to balance and improve your hormone profile. It most definitely increases sex drive and sex energy, and probably performance (016)
- A lot of studies, and a lot of anecdotal evidence, from clients and friends to anywhere online: most people are really pretty big fans (016)

## For the brain: the top four nootropics
- A nootropic is basically anything that's really a cognitive enhancer, enhancing brain power. Technically there are a couple of qualifications, but any cognitive enhancer is generally called that. Four, grown to love over the years, made a meaningful difference in the brain personally, and "I'll bet" they can in yours too (022)
- All four are essentially natural. Some drugs enhance the brain too, like a class called racetams, quite common in various forms, especially as you dig online into the world of nootropics, but sticking with natural solutions is highly recommended. **Story:** racetams and other synthetic nootropics were tried; they seem to suffer from what synthetic forms usually do: natural forms are simply much better for us and tend to work better with our bodies (022)
- The four: Huperzine A, Alpha GPC, acetyl-L-carnitine and ginkgo biloba (022)
- Take any or all of the four: they're all safe in appropriate doses, even at pretty high crazy doses, which you don't need, since more is not necessarily better (022)
- Timing of the four (022)
  - Ginkgo is the only one of the four really recommended at night: before bed is another awesome time. It can be taken pretty much whenever; personally, three times a day, usually with each of the two cups of coffee mixed with supplements, and once again before bed (022)
  - Some people take Huperzine A before bed, which will definitely bring more vivid dreams and potentially lucid dreaming; personally, it kind of works a little better earlier in the day (022)
  - Alpha GPC and acetyl-L-carnitine: definitely recommended earlier in the day, first thing, then maybe midday or afternoon, whenever you have that second cup of coffee (022)
- Personally, these are the top four brain-boosting supplements ever found, after trying lots of others, the favorites: highly recommended to give them a shot (022)

## Huperzine A
- Huperzine A, extracted from moss. It basically boosts acetylcholine in the brain, a very key neurotransmitter that directly impacts brain capacity: learning capacity, memory, processing speed, reaction time. Instead of being a source of choline, it stops choline's breakdown, keeping it active, so it effectively raises our levels (022)
- Personally, incredible benefits taken first thing in the morning. Really cheap, works really well: highly recommended (022)
- Very tiny doses, measured in micrograms, not milligrams: the recommended dose is anywhere between 50 and 150 micrograms a day; try that and see how you feel (022)
- It's often in a lot of brain-boosting blends, and often way overdosed: four or five hundred micrograms stuck in one is just too much. Various dosages were tried, and as with a lot of things in life, more is not necessarily better, definitely with huperzine. Really not a fan of supplement blends in general, for a lot of reasons, and this highlights it: get it directly. Easy to get, all over Amazon, quality brands in local health food stores; very cheap and very effective (022)

## Ginkgo biloba
- Ginkgo biloba: really all four benefit the body in so many ways besides the brain. Ginkgo works by enhancing circulation, especially to the brain, but also the rest of the body; enhancing it so significantly to certain parts of the brain gives massive brain-boosting effects, specifically on memory (022)
- Personally, acetyl-L-carnitine, Alpha GPC and even huperzine definitely bring a lot of processing speed, reaction time and learning capacity; ginkgo, a significant difference in memory, and the studies are really strong around memory too (022)
- Hormone-boosting supplements need cycling; others like ginkgo don't necessarily: you could pretty much take it regularly forever and be fine, at a moderate, appropriate dose. **Story:** cycling off things occasionally anyway, partially for the benefit and the sort of fun of feeling them fresh and new again. Back on ginkgo after a break, more really vivid memories from the past would pop into the brain: the past kind of lit up (022)
- Beyond memory, the enhanced circulation helps lots of aspects of the brain, and the body: it enhances nitric oxide, really important for blood flow, including for erections and exercise performance, and oxygen uptake. Some studies even show significantly improved skin moisture, "I would imagine" because of circulation to the skin (022)
- Ginkgo is absolutely worth taking, especially as we get a little older: a memory all of a sudden better than it has been recently is awesome (022)
- Dose: around 120 milligrams one to three times a day. It typically comes in 60 or 120 milligram capsules, the dosage a lot of the studies and brands use (022)
