> **In short** — Fasting is such a powerful tool against resistance. Intermittent fasting personally transformed health and fitness more than any other way of fueling: testosterone goes down when you eat, and digestion is really the biggest energy draw. Ease in by skipping breakfast, then shorten your eating window, earlier in the day, avoid eating really late, and still eat good fuel.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## Why it matters
- Fasting is such a powerful tool against resistance, like turning on a "temporary superpower against resistance" (001)
- Of the many ways to fuel your body, intermittent fasting, discovered just a few years ago, personally transformed health and fitness more than any other, and seems to have made the very most impact on life, body and mind too. **Story:** it came after years of trial and error with so many things nutritionally, first to lose fat and look a little better while overweight, then to feel better, be in shape and be strong (008)
- As it turns out, fasting has amazing benefits, even for just hours a day, giving your body a break from digesting food (008)
  - Your testosterone immediately goes down when you eat, so intermittent fasting is incredibly powerful for supporting and boosting your hormones (008)
  - Digestion is really the biggest energy draw on your body: it takes tremendous energy. **Story:** energy went up tremendously once fasting was part of the daily routine (008)
- Personally, the benefits are really wide and varied: more energy, a simpler life with fewer meals, putting on muscle and keeping off fat easier: everything about a shorter eating window has been incredibly beneficial (008)
- If there's one thing nutritionally to try, it's intermittent fasting. There's so much science now, but the bottom line: "Intermittent fasting just works", and it will probably, honestly, benefit your life (008)
- Intermittent fasting is so powerful for how you feel, burning fat, keeping your metabolism up, your energy levels and building muscle really easily, and it works especially well for men, though it does work well for women also (008)
- One of fasting's amazing benefits is the mitochondria boost, which happens especially after we break the fast, one of its fundamental powerful effects (022)

## How it started
- **Story:** once a 7-Eleven-hot-dog-for-breakfast guy who didn't exercise, absolutely miserable, eating complete toxic nonsense, then a journey from about as low as you can get physically, mentally and emotionally, at least as it felt, to the strongest and best ever, at almost 40 (008)
- **Story:** for years of training, in pretty decent shape, ideas from various sources meant eating all the time, every two hours or whatever, and reaching 11 or 12 or one o'clock unfed brought almost a guilty feeling: "oh man, I should have eaten". Then, as it was kind of just working, without real hunger, came rolling with it, and the eating period kept shrinking. Intermittent fasting, less known then, was personally not even heard of, nor were its benefits known: it just worked, which led to investigating why (008)

## How to start
- Ease into it gradually, so your body adapts. **Story:** eating now typically happens in about a two- to four-hour period each day, a fast of 20 to 22 hours. That's not a suggestion to jump at once from eating from breakfast until evening to only two or four hours (008)
- Eating from soon after waking until later, you're really not fasting except while you sleep. The best start is cutting out breakfast: you're already fasting from the evening, so start eating at noonish, with lunch, for at least a few hours in a true fasting state (008)
- It takes about 12 hours for your body to kind of shift gears into certain really beneficial processes. Stop eating at 7 p.m. and eat breakfast at 7 or 8 a.m., and you start eating just as those amazing benefits would begin. Skipping breakfast gives you at least a few hours a day past that point, enough to start feeling the benefits (008)
- A promise: you'll adapt quicker, and probably more easily, than you think. For a lot of people used to eating basically all their waking hours, fasting can seem kind of rough, but your body will adapt (008)
- If you haven't tried it, it very potentially could change your life from a nutritional fuel standpoint, so working towards it is highly encouraged: skip breakfast every day for a week and see how you feel and how long you take to adapt. Then, over time, in general, the shorter and earlier in the day your eating window, the better you'll feel (008)

## The eating window
- The next step is your eating window: from when you start eating until you stop for the day. Breakfast by 8 a.m. and eating until 8 p.m., hopefully not later, is a 12-hour window. Cut it back more and more for more benefit: in personal experience, the shorter the window, the better the results (008)
- If there's one other thing to avoid food-wise, it's eating really late at night: it hurts your hormones and kills your morning wood (008)

## What to eat in the window
- One caveat: still pay attention to what you fuel with during your window, especially as you cut it shorter, if you go that way: eat effectively, the right mix to support your activity and body (008)
  - Training really hard, you sometimes have to really pay attention to eating enough (008)
  - Not training super hard and maybe trying to keep extra weight off, it can still mean watching that you maybe don't eat too much, or some of the junkier things (008)
  - The eating window is amazingly powerful, but not a complete magic bullet: four hours a day of fast food, and you're screwed. The fundamentals of eating good fuel still apply (008)
