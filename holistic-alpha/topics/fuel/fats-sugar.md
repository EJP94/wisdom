*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## Good fats
- For testosterone, enough good fats, saturated and monounsaturated, from clean sources: coconut oil, olive oil, organic and sustainably produced palm oil, pasture-raised butter, ghee. They're incredibly important for your brain, and what your body produces hormones from. One big mistake a lot of people make is avoiding fat, and so not getting enough good fats. Of the healthy nuts, macadamia nuts and pumpkin seeds are, personally, two of the only ones really liked, for their fat profile and some other things (002)

## Toxic fats
- Avoid toxic fats "like the plague": basically the polyunsaturated fats, very unstable, which can very easily and will harm your body and hormones. Fried and fast food are absolutely chock full of them, as are a lot of processed and packaged foods. Peanut, canola, vegetable and sunflower oil, among a lot of these oils, are a really potent source (002)

## Sugar
- Sugar is absolutely huge, with a direct, significant negative impact on testosterone. In one study, men given sugary syrup saw their testosterone drop 25% and stay lower for hours after. Daily sugar is absolutely going to impact your hormones, and already does every time you consume meaningful amounts. Avoid it like the plague (002)

## Healthy ways to eat
- If morning wood is missing, the nutrition basics: enough healthy fats, no bad fats, no sugar, which have a big impact for sure. There seem to be a lot of different healthy ways to eat, and none include sugar or the toxic polyunsaturated fats of fast food, which can and will crush your hormones (011)
