## Alcohol
- **Story:** over a decade of personally testing dozens of things, because we all want some kind of edge, no matter how we're performing. Some years early in adulthood were spent just crushing hormones: really an alcoholic, drinking IPAs every single day, and feeling a hormonal impact from those hops (016)
- There's a reason men who drink way too much beer look the way they do: not just calories, but hormones, because hops are super estrogenic. To optimize hormones, cut beer at minimum to the absolute minimal, or better yet none: hard for some, but it changes the way you feel (016)
- Drinking, among what has a negative effect on dopamine: see [[dopamine]] (016)

## Planning your fuel
- For nutrition, maybe 15 minutes at the start of the day in the kitchen, seeing what you have and strategizing the fuel that will fuel you effectively and what to buy at the store. Decide in your mind how you'll fuel your body today and more often than not you'll follow through, if not every single time then a pretty good chunk of the time, and change your life and your body. It might take only 45 seconds, but at first maybe you do need the timer, with pen and paper if that's how you roll, or your phone. Strategizing instead of winging it throughout the day gets dramatically different results (019)
