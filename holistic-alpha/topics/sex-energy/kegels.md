## How to find them
- To feel them: stop the flow mid-pee, and the muscles doing it are a Kegel. Trying to pee harder is a reverse Kegel (006, 014)
  - Two sides of the same coin, flexing those muscles in different ways. Of the couple of different muscles there, it takes a little time to dial in which one you're using, especially if you've never practiced. In edging or sex, which muscle you use and how, in either direction, has an incredible impact on your arousal level, your ability to control it, and whether it rises or drops (014)

## What they work: the pelvic floor

The pelvic floor is in so many ways one of the sort of life force centers of our body and spirit, where one of the chakras essentially sits, and its state and condition is a fundamental driving power. Basically our sex energy core, and in a lot of ways our life force core, so exercising it is incredibly important (014)

Strengthening that whole area makes a tremendous difference in your sex life, absolutely, and in so many ways it really boosts sort of your core energy and life force (018)

## Which muscle: focus on the rear
- Focus on the rear: different ways of doing Kegels have dramatically different impacts, especially on your sex life. Three muscles are involved: the bulbocavernosus (BC), the pubococcygeus (PC) and the iliococcygeus (IC). The technical names don't really matter: what matters is where they sit and which you focus on (018)
  - Going from the front to the back, and up as you go back: the BC is the most in front, kind of under your balls; the PC is in the middle; the IC sits further back, against the coccyx bone, so when you squeeze it you can almost feel it in your super low back (018)
  - Focusing forward, on the PC and especially the BC, is much less effective for controlling your arousal level, with a much different impact on your sex life than the deeper, rear muscles. Personally found: the deeper and more towards the back, the bigger the muscles, with greater control; the smaller BC and PC a lot of times don't give that ability, and without the biggest, most powerful muscles, the IC towards the rear, you don't get the same impact (018)
  - So, highly suggested: still imagine stopping the flow of urine, or holding in a fart, but feel the squeeze towards the back, against the coccyx bone in your super low back, the ideal place. That gives much greater control over your arousal, and sort of a bigger life energy impact than the BC, just under your nuts (018)
- A reverse Kegel squeezes the same muscles the other way, ideally again towards the rear, though you really want to exercise all of them: imagine pushing out a fart or peeing harder. Mixing both is a whole different world from Kegels alone, a huge difference (018)
