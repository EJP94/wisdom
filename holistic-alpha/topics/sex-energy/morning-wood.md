## What it is and why it happens
- Morning wood relates to our sex power, and it is also an amazing indicator of what's going on in our bodies overall (011)
- Why it happens: most hormone production happens at night, then our hormones, testosterone included, spike to their highest of the day, usually between 5:30 and 8:30 AM. That spike gives us morning wood, which rocks (011)

## A body indicator
- If things are kind of going okay with your body, nutrition and hormones, we should be and will be getting morning wood on a regular basis, absolutely on a daily basis. A lot of different things can impact it. When it all of a sudden stops, comes some days and not others, or changes long term, you used to always get it and don't anymore, something is going on that can be optimized and improved: listen to your body and don't ignore it. Even at older ages, with nutrition, sex energy and hormones in a healthy place, you can and will still get it (011)

  - Sex energy: ejaculating too often drains all of your energy, and your morning wood can be a direct signal of it, depending on your age and hormonal circumstance. Pay attention and very likely you might notice no morning wood the next morning after you ejaculate, or the next two or four mornings. Maybe you still get it if you're younger and your hormones are in really good shape, but very likely that's kind of a sign too (011)

- Probably, for the most part, barring some sort of pre-existing condition, at a wide variety of ages, once those 80/20 things are locked in we should be getting morning wood: "It's as simple as that." (011)

## Harness it
- Harness morning wood: a strong belief that we should use it. Our body is kind of offering it up, and by using it we probably want to signal back that it's something good we want more of. With a girlfriend, partner or wife, morning sex is an extremely good idea; without, spend a few minutes stimulating yourself. Either way it's a positive feedback loop, just don't ejaculate: it fuels kind of the rest of your day and the next morning's morning wood. Don't just notice it and go about your day: take some time and utilize it (011)
