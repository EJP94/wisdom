## What ejaculating does
- Avoid excess ejaculation: too often, no matter how, it drains your hormones, your life energy, your "qi, as some people call it", in major ways (002)
- The moment you ejaculate, alone or with a woman, you basically hit the reset button: your sexual energy goes back to sort of your baseline, as if nothing had been built. That varies a little with your hormones and situation, but you're back at essentially square one. Ejaculating too often, especially alone, takes you well below it and starts to suck life out of you (006)
- Building your sex energy instead of wasting it, by not ejaculating too often, is an 80/20 thing with a tremendous impact on your overall results. Do pretty much everything else quote-unquote right, but ejaculate too often and "you're screwed", "that's the way it is". No morning wood while ejaculating on a really regular basis: maybe think about building that energy instead of letting it leave you (011)

## Ejaculating alone
- Stop ejaculating alone, which has the worst impact on your hormones: avoid it ever if at all possible, or do it very, very infrequently (002)
  - Like porn, jerking off to the point of ejaculation has to go. The difference between cutting it out and allowing it is absolutely ginormous (006)
- Partner or not, single or divorced, no ejaculating alone and no porn always apply: ejaculating alone and porn seem to be just fundamental things that suck the life out of you (006)
- Jerking off daily probably takes less than 15 minutes; to the point of ejaculation, in kind of a negative way where you lose that energy, almost guaranteed probably less. Set a timer: your only job for those 15 minutes is to eliminate it. Day after day, your internal power and fire completely change. You don't have to do it all at once (019)

## Separating orgasm and ejaculation
- Be active and fuel your sexual energy, with one partner, yourself or multiple partners. It doesn't mean not having sex. Far enough along, it means separating orgasm and ejaculation, so you can be highly sexually active (in general, not with multiple people, though that's your choice). You can absolutely work towards it, and the more you conserve semen, the more you feel it in your hormones, life force energy and mind. The encouragement: learn to ride the edge of not ejaculating while getting the benefits of sexual activity, and eventually to separate ejaculation and orgasm (002)
- In a relationship, the tremendous power is in controlling ejaculation with your partner too. You can absolutely reach 10 and have an amazing, longer-lasting orgasm without ejaculating, and end with incredible energy and power, way above baseline instead of back at square one. The more you do it, the more it builds. It takes practice for sure, and intention (006)
- With a woman and regular sex, the goal still seems to be separating orgasm and ejaculation, which takes practice (006)
