> **In short** — Retention means conserving semen: avoid excess ejaculation and, above all, ejaculating alone, without giving up sex. The way, it seems: masturbate and edge without ejaculating and, with practice, separate orgasm from ejaculation. Later: retention is not for everybody, nor about never releasing; an aware, intentional release can be powerful. Emotional walls are very common; the suggested approach is introspection.

*Ideas from Holistic Alpha, as told by host Steven Mathis and, where marked, his guests and listeners. Numbers in parentheses are episodes. Sources he credits are named where they appear. Not fact-checked.*

## What it is and is not

- Retention is "actually a journey of self-exploration on very deep levels", and it's all beautiful (675) <!-- 675-38 -->
  - For most guys in the beginning, there's not really a sense that retention is "actually a journey of self-exploration on very deep levels" (675) <!-- 675-37 -->
- A lot of guys, it seems, start retention, probably personally too, from a little more of a superficial place, so to speak: maybe improving erection quality, or maybe shame (675) <!-- 675-36 -->
- Fueling your sexual energy doesn't mean not having sex (002) <!-- 002-36 -->
- Cutting out jerking off to the point of ejaculation doesn't mean not masturbating or exploring your sexuality alone, which is highly encouraged (006) <!-- 006-16 -->
  - Masturbating or exploring your sexuality alone is encouraged especially if you're single, to stoke your own fire, and probably in a relationship too, since positive practice makes you better with a partner, in performance, sexual energy and sexual health (006) <!-- 006-17 -->
  - When masturbating or exploring your sexuality alone, you don't ejaculate (006) <!-- 006-18 -->
- **Guest:** There was no benefit really to the NoFap mentality of "don't even touch yourself", beyond trying to get you to break an addiction (537) <!-- 537-22 -->
- **Guest (Rick):** A lot of guys in semen retention are confused into thinking holding on as long as possible is the goal, so ejaculating means failing (565) <!-- 565-32 -->
- **Guest (Rick):** Personally, his philosophy on retention shifted to "hold on and if it happens, it does"; if it doesn't, you're moving forward, you've gained a lot, you're expanding yourself (565) <!-- 565-33 -->

## What ejaculation does

- The moment you ejaculate, alone or with a woman, you basically hit the reset button: your sexual energy goes back to sort of your baseline, as if nothing had been built (006) <!-- 006-21 -->
  - The reset after ejaculation varies a little with your hormones and situation, but you're back at essentially square one (006) <!-- 006-22 -->
- Avoid excess ejaculation: too often, no matter how, it drains your hormones, your life energy, your "qi, as some people call it", in major ways (002) <!-- 002-33 -->
  - Ejaculating too often, especially alone, takes you well below baseline and starts to suck life out of you (006) <!-- 006-23 -->
  - Too many releases will drag you down; unconscious ones, without intention, discipline or awareness, probably will (675) <!-- 675-22 -->

## Why retain

- The more you conserve semen, the more you feel it in your hormones, life force energy and mind (002) <!-- 002-39 -->
- The difference you'll feel on a fundamental level from the practice of not ejaculating is tremendous (006) <!-- 006-42 -->
  - Emotionally, too, as both host and guest know, retention makes a huge difference in their emotions (the guest agrees) (549) <!-- 549-66 -->
- One amazing benefit of edging without ejaculating: not letting yourself quite reach that point builds willpower (006) <!-- 006-28 -->

## Ejaculating alone

- Stop ejaculating alone, which has the worst impact on your hormones: avoid it ever if at all possible, or do it very, very infrequently (002) <!-- 002-34 -->
  - For sex power, jerking off to the point of ejaculation has to go (006) <!-- 006-14 -->
  - The difference between cutting out jerking off to the point of ejaculation and allowing it is absolutely ginormous (006) <!-- 006-15 -->
  - Partner or not, single or divorced, no ejaculating alone and no porn always apply: ejaculating alone and porn seem to be just fundamental things that suck the life out of you (006) <!-- 006-39 -->
  - **Later view (2024):** Retention is not about never releasing, nor about seeing a release as a bad thing, "because it's not" (675) <!-- 675-21 -->

## Separating orgasm and ejaculation

- Far enough along, fueling your sexual energy means separating orgasm and ejaculation, so you can be highly sexually active (in general, not with multiple people, though that's your choice) (002) <!-- 002-37 -->
- You can absolutely work towards separating orgasm and ejaculation (002) <!-- 002-38 -->
  - You can absolutely reach 10 on the arousal scale and have an amazing, longer-lasting orgasm without ejaculating, and end with incredible energy and power, way above baseline instead of back at square one (006) <!-- 006-30 -->
- The more you reach orgasm without ejaculating, the more the energy builds (006) <!-- 006-31 -->

## How to practice

- The Holistic Alpha way, it seems, single or not: masturbate and edge regularly, highly recommended, but absolutely don't ejaculate (006) <!-- 006-26 -->
  - Masturbating and edging without ejaculating takes practice and willpower, especially if you've always ejaculated since you were a kid (006) <!-- 006-27 -->
  - **Later view (2024):** An aware, intentional release at the right time for the right reasons can be incredibly powerful and beneficial (675) <!-- 675-23 -->
    - Through introspection (meditating, thinking, journaling, talking to a close friend), once clearly aware of something you really need to let go of, an intentional release aimed at letting it go "can be incredibly powerful" (675) <!-- 675-16 -->
    - With clear awareness of something you need to let go of and a clear intention, you'll very likely experience a lot of positive benefits from an intentional release (675) <!-- 675-17 -->
  - **Later view (2024):** With awareness of deeper things to let go of, releasing is probably a good call, if that's what you feel (675) <!-- 675-26 -->
- The encouragement: learn to ride the edge of not ejaculating while getting the benefits of sexual activity, and eventually to separate ejaculation and orgasm (002) <!-- 002-40 -->
  - After the three fundamental shifts, you practice edging more and more, get better at controlling arousal, practice Kegels and reverse Kegels and get stronger, and after some practice you can reach a full-on, super powerful level 10 orgasm without ejaculating (006) <!-- 006-66 -->
  - To move towards separating orgasm and ejaculation and gain control, practice deep breathing (006) <!-- 006-46 -->
- Orgasm without ejaculating takes practice for sure, and intention (006) <!-- 006-32 -->
  - A level 10 orgasm without ejaculating doesn't come in three days: more important is shifting how you look at it and when you allow yourself to ejaculate, which has a huge impact (006) <!-- 006-67 -->

## Releasing

- "Releasing" is a key word: ejaculation is in fact a huge energetic release, often thought of, maybe not negatively (though sometimes it's easy to fall into that trap), but as draining (675) <!-- 675-14 -->
  - Ejaculation is also a powerful potential release of things we want to let go of (675) <!-- 675-15 -->

## The emotional wall

- **Listener question:** A young man asks whether others, in their second to third week of retaining, hit a phase of feeling bad about themselves and their progress and just feel like releasing. He learned somewhere that this is when unhealed traumas and inner child problems arise: is it true, and how to deal with it, since it can be hard and overwhelming, especially alone? (675) <!-- 675-01 -->
- In retention, probably the easy way to call a phase of feeling bad and just feeling like releasing is "a bit of an emotional wall", kind of hit in some way after maybe feeling great, better and better (675) <!-- 675-04 -->
- Hitting an emotional wall in retention: "It is very, very common" at various stages of retention (675) <!-- 675-05 -->
  - In retention, you will face these emotional walls (675) <!-- 675-32 -->
- Don't get too hung up on the time frame of the emotional wall in retention: it may happen at any point, probably around a week for a lot of guys starting out, or a month or two in for those practicing that long; maybe why one listener hits it at two to three weeks (675) <!-- 675-03 -->
- For a lot of guys in the beginning of retention, the wall is an overwhelm of sexual energy, of uncontrolled lust (675) <!-- 675-06 -->
- A wall of feeling bad about yourself and your progress in retention is a little bit different from an overwhelm of sexual energy, and very, very important (675) <!-- 675-07 -->
- For a lot of guys, retention is practiced essentially alone, so the emotional wall can feel very alone and unsure (675) <!-- 675-10 -->

## Why the wall comes

- Do unhealed traumas and inner child problems arise in retention? "I would say absolutely one of the things that will happen as you practice retention": you dive deeper into yourself, your power, life force and emotions, so you'll hit points where you get a little uncomfortable and emotions you haven't really tapped into before (675) <!-- 675-08 -->
  - Hitting uncomfortable points and emotions you haven't tapped into before in retention is to be expected: experienced personally, and seen "over and over and over again with guys", one listener being an example, along with many clients and others heard from (675) <!-- 675-09 -->
- Emotions living in the body is one reason we hit the emotional wall in retention in different ways, times and layers, tapping more strongly into the life force there (675) <!-- 675-19 -->
- **Story:** A promise: 10-plus years into the retention journey, emotional walls still come up, in different ways, layers, depths and time frames, because this is how we function. "And it ought to still come up." (675) <!-- 675-33 -->
- Emotional walls still coming up in retention is a good thing: you're tuning into deeper levels of yourself (675) <!-- 675-34 -->

## How to meet the wall

- Suggested approach to the emotional wall in retention: base it on a lot of introspection (675) <!-- 675-11 -->
- At the emotional wall, releasing won't always necessarily be the best call, and there's not a right or wrong: "it's all testing and learning" (675) <!-- 675-24 -->
  - There is "not a one size fits all approach" to the emotional wall in retention: every individual and every moment is unique (675) <!-- 675-28 -->
- Hitting the emotional wall doesn't necessarily mean releasing is right: sometimes the best call is to keep retaining and lean into those emotions, feel them, sit with them, meditate, go for a walk; don't resist them, be with them (675) <!-- 675-27 -->
  - At the emotional wall, sometimes the work is to keep pushing against retention while working through emotions in other ways, probably especially without clarity about what to let go of, or when it isn't deep and longstanding, not stress from work yesterday (675) <!-- 675-25 -->
- At the emotional wall in retention, with intention, patience, grace for yourself, discipline and commitment, ask whether something clearly has been eating at you for a while and this is a positive opportunity to let it go. If so, go for it: set the intention (675) <!-- 675-29 -->
- At the emotional wall in retention, if nothing clearly has been eating at you for a while to let go of, most of the time, keep retaining: especially earlier in the journey, a big part of retention is pushing up against that edge, a little farther and farther (675) <!-- 675-30 -->
- Even so, don't beat yourself up if or when you release: it's never a bad thing, always part of the journey and a learning experience, even if you might learn, having felt a little sort of on that edge and gone for it anyway, to be a little more disciplined next time (675) <!-- 675-31 -->
- The emotional wall may be uncomfortable, new or unwanted, but go with the roller coaster, the climbs and drops, as long as you keep stepping with awareness, intention and heart (675) <!-- 675-35 -->
