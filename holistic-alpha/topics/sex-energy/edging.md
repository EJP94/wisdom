## What it is
- Riding the edge of not ejaculating while getting the benefits of sexual activity: see [[semen-retention]] (002)
- Edging, as a lot of people refer to it: on an arousal scale of 1 to 10, where 10 is ejaculating, you spend time alone without reaching 10, maybe nine or nine and a half, eight or seven, harnessing the energy, feeling the pleasure and fueling your internal fire. An edging session builds your power, even if you're single (006)

## Alone, without ejaculating
- Cutting out ejaculating alone doesn't mean not masturbating or exploring your sexuality alone, which is highly encouraged: especially if you're single, to stoke your own fire, and probably in a relationship too, since positive practice makes you better with a partner, in performance, sexual energy and sexual health. But you don't ejaculate (006)
- Masturbating and edging regularly without ejaculating, as the way to build sexual energy: see [[life-force]] (006)
- Masturbating and edging without ejaculating takes practice and willpower, especially if you've always ejaculated since you were a kid, and one amazing benefit is just that: not letting yourself quite reach that point builds willpower (006)

## Control: breathing and Kegels
- Two big things help you gain control, in edging without ejaculating and in reaching 10 with incredible, powerful orgasms without ejaculating, with a partner or alone: breathing, and Kegels and reverse Kegels, which exercise your sex muscles and, with the right breathing techniques, let you start to control your arousal level (006)
- Which muscle you use, and reverse Kegels, for controlling arousal and lasting longer: see [[kegels]] (014, 018)
- A focused, quiet mind, and controlling your arousal level: see [[meditation]] (020)

## Spending time in 5 to 9
- Most of us, most of our lives, have a habit a lot of people seem to get into as kids: arousal goes from like four to 10 on a linear, fast scale, you ejaculate and that's it, alone or with a woman. The fundamental shift is spending time in five, six, seven, eight, nine, going kind of up and down (006)
  - Especially with a partner: get to eight or nine, bring it back down to five or six, let it rise and bring it down again, and your performance goes up powerfully, in how long you last and the pleasure you create for your partner (006)
  - Alone, the pleasure is so much more in that range than skyrocketing to 10, and boom, you're drained and done (006)
- You won't be perfect: you might slip over the edge unintentionally. Don't feel guilty or bummed, it happens: be intentional and get back on the practice. The difference you'll feel on a fundamental level is tremendous (006)
